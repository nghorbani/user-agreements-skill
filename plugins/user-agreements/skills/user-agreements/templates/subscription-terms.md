# Subscription terms

Law as of: 2026-10-20

A fill-in template for a recurring-payment subscription: monthly or annual, with or without a free
trial or an introductory price, delivered as a digital service, digital content, or goods supplied on
a recurring basis. It assembles the clauses a subscription needs in the order a user meets them: how
the contract forms, what the user pays and how, how the term runs and renews, how the user cancels,
what happens if either side wants to change the deal, and the standing terms (content rights,
liability, disputes, notices) that sit underneath the whole relationship. Build the final document by
keeping the module for each jurisdiction the subscription actually reaches and dropping the rest; do
not ship every module to every user.

## How to fill this in

- Work out first which of three counterparty types signs up: an EU consumer, an EU business customer,
  or a US user (consumer or business). Each clause below states a module for each type; keep only the
  modules this subscription's actual audience needs, and state plainly, in the finished document,
  which law applies to which user rather than leaving one blended text for everyone.
- Work out what the subscription actually supplies: a digital service kept running (streaming,
  software-as-a-service, a hosted account), digital content supplied as a one-off file or item inside
  a subscription wrapper, or physical goods shipped on a recurring schedule. The warranty and
  withdrawal clauses below branch on this; fill in only the bracketed paragraph that matches what is
  actually sold and drop the other.
- Confirm whether the business or any affiliate sits in one of the exempt categories the California
  and New York auto-renewal statutes list before drafting the US module; an exempt business carries
  none of those states' duties at all (`references/clauses/term-renewal-and-cancellation.md`,
  checklists/subscription-auto-renewal.md Scope section).
- Confirm the contract is not a distance financial-services contract before drafting the EU
  withdrawal module from this template; that contract type runs on a different information list and
  withdrawal period this template does not state (`references/clauses/withdrawal-eu.md`).
- Where the subscription is sold through a third-party platform (an app store, a marketplace) rather
  than the business's own checkout, flag as an open point whether the same-medium-as-consent duty in
  California and New York, and the German cancellation-button duty, can be secured from the platform
  operator by contract; the clause files raise this without settling it.
- Fill every bracketed placeholder ([trader name], [the service], [price], [initial term], [renewal
  term], and so on) from the actual product before publishing; do not ship a bracket in a live
  document.
- Where this template points to `references/prescribed-wording.md` for a labelled control (PW-DE-1 to
  PW-DE-5, PW-EU-2 to PW-EU-10), copy that wording exactly as that file gives it; do not improvise a
  substitute label.

## Module: acceptance and sign-up

By [clicking the button labeled [exact button label] / continuing to [the action that completes
sign-up or checkout]], you agree to these Terms and to our Privacy Policy.

[Link to the full terms, placed immediately next to this sentence and the control it names, in a
color and weight that contrasts with the surrounding text.]

Before you complete your order, you can review and change the items in your cart, your subscription
plan and your payment method. Once you submit the order, we will send you an electronic confirmation
to the email address you provided.

We will keep a copy of these terms and your order details and make them available to you on request.
This agreement, your signature and your order are not denied legal effect only because they are in
electronic form.

**EU consumers.** Immediately before the order button, and with nothing else between the two, add the
four-point disclosure the button must sit next to: the technical steps to conclude the contract,
whether we file the concluded contract and whether you can access it, the technical means to spot and
correct an input error before you submit, and the languages the contract can be concluded in.
**Germany.** The order button carries only PW-DE-1 ("zahlungspflichtig bestellen") or an equally
unambiguous equivalent, with nothing else on it; the price, the plan's main characteristics, the
initial and renewal term, and the rest of the EGBGB Art. 246a list sit directly before that button,
highlighted, not behind a link. **US.** Place the assent sentence on the same screen as the control it
names, in a size and color comparably prominent to the surrounding text, directly next to the control,
visible without scrolling. Where sign-up spans more than one screen, repeat the notice-and-control
pairing on the screen where the subscription itself is actually started; do not rely on a notice shown
earlier in the flow. Do not use a pre-checked assent box.

The total price for [the subscription] is [price], including all taxes, for each [billing period].
[Where an introductory or trial price applies: for the first [period], the price is [intro price];
after that it is [standard price], as stated below.]

We accept payment by [payment methods]. [Where any method carries a fee: using [payment method] costs
an extra [amount], which reflects what it costs us to accept it.] [Germany: a customary, reasonable,
free payment option remains available alongside any surcharged method.]

We will not charge you anything beyond the price above unless you have separately and expressly agreed
to it. We do not treat your failure to uncheck a box, or your silence, as that agreement.

**EU consumers.** State the total price inclusive of all taxes for each billing period before you are
bound; for a fixed-rate plan running for an indefinite period or one that renews automatically, state

The references here do not cover a US federal or state duty on this single-transaction price
disclosure; see the term, renewal and cancellation module below for the US auto-renewal price rules
that do apply to a subscription specifically.

## Module: term, renewal and cancellation

This agreement starts on the date [trader name] confirms your order and continues for [initial term].
Unless you cancel before the end of that term, it then renews automatically for successive [renewal
term] periods, at the price and on the terms in effect at the time of each renewal, until you cancel
or we end it as this clause allows.

Before you are charged for the first time, and before each renewal, we will show you, or send you, the
price you will be charged, how often you will be charged, and how to cancel.

You can cancel at any time. Cancelling stops the next renewal; it does not refund a period that has
already started unless this agreement or the law says otherwise. [Where a cancellation during an
already-paid period still gets a partial refund: add the refund terms here.]

**EU consumers.** `references/eu/uctd.md` sets the EU-wide floor: an automatic renewal needs a
reasonable, not unreasonably early, objection deadline, and cancellation is at least as easy as sign-up.
Outside Germany, the specific term lengths and notice periods below are not separately stated for every
member state; treat the gap as an open point (see Open points to raise). **EU business customers.** The
references do not set a business-specific renewal or cancellation position; treat a business-facing
renewal and cancellation clause as an open point for counsel. **Germany.** State the renewal mechanism
so it extends the contract onto an indefinite term, not a further fixed term, and gives you a
contractual right to end the renewed contract at any time on no more than one month's notice. Where the
contract instead binds you to an initial term, a silent-renewal clause, or a termination notice period
before the end of the initial term, keep the initial term at two years or less and the pre-end notice
period at one month or less.

[Germany, online continuing contract: add the cancellation-button paragraph below this module.] Provide
a cancellation button, permanently available, immediately and easily accessible, labelled PW-DE-2
("Verträge hier kündigen"), routing directly to a confirmation page labelled with the PW-DE-3
("jetzt kündigen") confirmation button. The confirmation page asks only for: cancellation type and, for
an extraordinary cancellation, the reason; data identifying you; data identifying the contract; an
optional end date you are never required to fill in; and an electronic channel for the confirmation.
You can store your own cancellation declaration on a durable medium, time-stamped. We confirm receipt
immediately, electronically, in text form, stating the content, the time stamp, and the resulting end
date. A missing or non-compliant button or confirmation page lets you cancel at any time without any
notice period, whether or not a cancellation right otherwise exists on the merits.

**US.** Before taking your billing information, we disclose, in visual proximity to the control that
records your consent: that the plan continues until cancelled, the cancellation mechanism, the charge
and whether and how it may change, the renewal term length, and any minimum-purchase obligation. Where
the plan starts with a free gift, trial, or a temporary price, we disclose the price that will apply
once that period ends. We obtain your affirmative consent to the offer terms before the first charge,
including for a promotional or discounted price, and keep a verifiable record of it. After consent, we
send a retainable acknowledgment covering the offer terms, the cancellation policy, and how to cancel.
We describe the cancellation mechanism: [a toll-free telephone number / an email address / a postal
address / another cost-effective, timely, easy-to-use method], reachable through at least the same
medium you used to consent. If online acceptance is offered, we add no step beyond permitted account
authentication that delays online cancellation. Where a material change, including a fee change, is
made, we give clear, conspicuous, retainable notice with cancellation instructions before it takes
effect; [New York: on a price increase, we either capture your affirmative consent to the new price
before charging it, or let you cancel within at least fourteen days after the charge with a pro rata
refund]. Nothing here interferes with, detracts from, or contradicts your ability to give affirmative
consent to renewal, and we do not misrepresent whether the plan renews automatically. A discount,
retention benefit, or cancellation-effect information is shown only together with the required
cancellation trigger, never instead of it or ahead of it.

## Module: changes to these terms

We may update these terms from time to time. Where a change is more than a minor clarification,
correction of an error, or adjustment required to keep the service lawful, safe or secure, we will
tell you about it in advance, state the reason for the change and when it takes effect, and give you a
reasonable period before the change applies. [EU consumers, digital content or digital service] Where
the change is more than a minor negative effect on your access to or use of the service, you may end
the contract free of charge within the notice period described above. A change does not apply to a
dispute that already exists between you and us before the change takes effect. We will not ask you to
accept a change through silence or continued use alone where the change affects a right you already
have or a payment you already make. If you do not agree to a change, your option is to stop using the
service and cancel your subscription under the term, renewal and cancellation module above, before the
change takes effect.

**EU consumers.** A term letting us change these terms without a valid, contract-stated reason is
itself unfair and void; so is a term letting us change a characteristic of the subscription without a
valid reason. Where the subscription is digital content or a digital service supplied over a period,
a change is permitted only where the contract itself allows it and states a valid reason, the change
carries no extra cost for you, you are told about it clearly, and, where it does more than minimally
and negatively affect your access or use, you are told reasonably in advance, on a durable medium,
with at least 30 days to terminate free of charge or keep the unmodified version instead. **Germany.**
A clause letting us change these terms, including price, through your silence or continued use, with
no substantive limit on what can be changed, is invalid, even where a matching special termination
right is also offered. **US.** No change applies to a dispute that has already accrued before the
change takes effect, and no change binds a person who has only browsed the service without
transacting.

*Legal basis: `references/clauses/changes-to-terms.md`.*

## Module: the right to withdraw (EU and Germany)

[Trader name] gives you 14 days to withdraw from this contract, without giving any reason and without
any cost beyond what this clause states.

The withdrawal period runs for 14 days from the day this contract is concluded. To withdraw, you tell
[trader name] of your decision by a clear statement, for example a letter sent by post, a fax, or an
email to [contact details]. You may use the withdrawal form at [link], but do not have to. Sending the
withdrawal statement before the period ends is enough to meet the deadline.

[Where the contract is concluded through an online interface: you may also withdraw through the
withdrawal function at [link or location], available at any time during the withdrawal period. Using
this function needs you to give or confirm your name, the details identifying this contract, and a way
of reaching you to confirm the withdrawal. A second, separate step then submits the declaration. Once
that second step is taken, we send an acknowledgment without delay, stating what was declared and when
it was received. For a German-facing online interface, the withdrawal function and the confirmation
control that submits the declaration carry the labels PW-DE-4 and PW-DE-5, or an equally unambiguous
equivalent for each.]

If you withdraw, we refund every payment received within 14 days of being told of the withdrawal, using
the same payment method you used, with no fee.

[For digital content not supplied on a tangible medium, or a digital service: if you asked for
performance to start before the withdrawal period ends, you pay a share of the agreed price for what
was supplied up to the point of withdrawal. You owe nothing for that early-started performance where we
did not get your express request and prior acknowledgment for the early start, or did not give you the
information on the withdrawal right and its costs that this clause describes.]

This right applies only where this subscription is not a distance financial-services contract; a
financial-services subscription runs on a separate information list and withdrawal period this clause
does not state (see Open points to raise).

**EU consumers.** Where the trader concludes the contract through an online interface, add the
bracketed withdrawal-function paragraph and label the two controls PW-EU-2 and PW-EU-3 (or PW-EU-7 and
PW-EU-8 for a German-facing document); this applies on top of the letter-or-email route, not instead of
it. Attach the complete model withdrawal information, correctly filled in, as the withdrawal-information
section of these terms or the order confirmation (PW-EU-4, or PW-EU-9 for a German-facing document).
Offer the model withdrawal form alongside it (PW-EU-5 or PW-EU-10) as something you may use, never as
the only valid way to withdraw. Where the trader omits this withdrawal-right information, the 14-day
period extends to 12 months from the end of the original period, then expires 14 (or 30) days after you
are later given that information. **EU business customers.** Drop this module for a business customer
acting in that capacity. **Germany.** Label the withdrawal function and its confirmation control
PW-DE-4 and PW-DE-5, or an equally unambiguous equivalent for each, for any distance contract concluded
through an online interface; see references/prescribed-wording.md, the PW-DE entries. Use the complete
Muster-Widerrufsbelehrung, PW-DE-6, filled in for the contract type, as the statutory safe harbor for
the withdrawal-information duty; a non-compliant or incomplete version does not discharge it. Offer the
Muster-Widerrufsformular, PW-DE-7, alongside it with
the trader's name, address and email already filled in, but never as the only accepted channel. Where
the trader sells through a third-party platform as well as its own site, both channels need the
withdrawal function. **US.** These reference files cover no US withdrawal or cooling-off right; do not
reuse this module's 14-day period, withdrawal function, or model form language for a US-facing document.

## Module: what the subscription delivers (warranty and conformity)

[Trader name] supplies [the digital content / the digital service / the goods] so that it matches what
this contract describes and promises, and, on top of that, has the quantity, quality, and performance a
consumer may reasonably expect for a subscription of this kind, including its usual functionality,
compatibility, durability, and security.

[For a continuously supplied digital content or digital service: [trader name] keeps [the subscription]
updated, including with security updates, for the period the contract runs, or the period a consumer
may reasonably expect for a subscription of this kind and purpose. [Trader name] tells you when an
update is available and what happens if it is not installed.]

If [the subscription] does not match what this clause promises, you can ask [trader name] to fix it.
[For goods delivered under the subscription: you choose between repair and replacement; trader name may
decline your choice only where it is impossible or clearly disproportionate, and must then offer the
other option instead. For digital content or a digital service, trader name has a free choice of how to
bring it into conformity.] The fix is free of charge, happens within a reasonable time, and causes you
no significant inconvenience. If the fix does not happen in time, fails, or trader name refuses or will
clearly not do it, you can ask instead for a price reduction, or can end the contract unless the problem
is only minor.

**EU consumers.** State the liability period as at least two years from delivery or supply; for a
subscription with continuous supply, extend it to the whole period the contract provides for supply if
that runs longer than two years. Keep repair and replacement free of charge, within a reasonable time,
without significant inconvenience. Termination is unavailable for a merely minor lack of conformity,
and the burden of proving the shortfall is minor sits with the trader. **Germany.** Use the German terms
for the two conformity layers (vertraglich vereinbarte Beschaffenheit and the objective requirements).
State the limitation period as two years from delivery for the general case, with the consumer-sale
layer adding further floors (twelve months after continuous digital-element provision ends, and the
rest the Legal basis file states). **US.** Draft a written warranty only where [trader name] chooses to
give one; caption it conspicuously as either "full (statement of duration) warranty" or "limited
warranty" (PW-US-FED-4), with no other designation.

## Module: liability and disclaimers

Nothing in these terms limits or excludes a liability that the law does not let [trader name] limit or
exclude. In particular, nothing here limits [trader name]'s liability for death or personal injury, for
its own fraud or intentional wrongdoing, for a grossly negligent breach of duty, for a negligent breach
of a duty essential to this contract, or for liability a specific statute states cannot be contracted
away.

Outside what the paragraph above protects, [trader name]'s liability for a negligent breach of a duty
that is not essential to this contract is limited to the loss that was foreseeable and ordinary for a
contract of this kind. [Trader name] is not liable for indirect or consequential loss, including lost
profit or lost data, except where the paragraph above says this clause cannot limit that liability.

**EU consumers.** State the floor first: nothing in this clause reaches liability for death or personal
injury caused by [trader name]'s own act or omission, or unduly excludes or limits your legal rights for
non-performance or defective performance. Write the clause in plain, intelligible language that lets you
work out its real economic consequences. Where [the subscription] involves a product within the scope of
the Product Liability Directive (a movable, including software, an AI system, or a digital manufacturing
file, placed on the market or put into service after 9 December 2026), state that nothing in these terms
limits or excludes [trader name]'s, or any other liable economic operator's, statutory liability to an
injured natural person under that Directive (Art. 15 Directive (EU) 2024/2853). **Germany.** No clause may exclude or limit liability for
death, personal injury, or harm to health arising from a negligent breach of duty by [trader name], or
an intentional or negligent breach by its staff or agents, with no carve-out for any contract type. No
clause may exclude or limit liability for other damage arising from a grossly negligent breach of duty,
except the statute's own narrow carve-outs. **US.** Draft a conspicuous as-is or as-available disclaimer
and a liability cap (a stated dollar figure, or the subscription fees paid in a defined period,
whichever is greater), paired with a disclaimer of incidental and consequential damages. For a
California subscriber, no release, disclaimer or limitation clause may exempt [trader name] from its
own fraud, from willful injury, or from a violation of law.

## Module: indemnity (business subscriptions)

[Drop this module for an EU consumer subscription; use it only for a business-to-business
subscription.]

[Counterparty name] covers [trader name] for a loss, cost, or claim a third party brings against
[trader name] because of: content [counterparty name] uploads, posts, or sends through [the
subscription]; [counterparty name]'s own breach of this contract; or [counterparty name]'s use of [the
subscription] in a way this contract does not allow. This covers [trader name]'s reasonable costs of
defending the claim and any amount [trader name] has to pay because of it, once the claim is confirmed
or settled with [counterparty name]'s agreement.

This does not cover a claim that comes from [trader name]'s own breach of this contract or negligence.
[Trader name] tells [counterparty name] promptly about a claim this clause might cover, lets
[counterparty name] take part in defending it, and does not settle it in a way that admits fault on
[counterparty name]'s part without [counterparty name]'s agreement.

[Add a stated cap on the amount covered and a stated time limit for bringing a claim under this clause,
set out in the pricing or order document the parties actually negotiate.]

**EU consumers.** Drop this module outright for an EU consumer subscription: an indemnity running from
the consumer toward the trader is tested under the significant-imbalance and good-faith standard and
typically falls within the Annex pattern of binding the consumer to obligations the trader itself does
not mirror. **Germany.** Drop this module for a consumer subscription for the same reason. An indemnity
clause that shifts onto the consumer the burden of proving a circumstance within the trader's own
sphere of responsibility is void outright. **EU business customers / US business.** Use the model
wording above; state a cap, a time limit, and a defense-and-control mechanic as negotiated terms.

*Legal basis: `references/clauses/indemnity.md`.*

## Module: moderation and suspension

We set out, in plain language, the restrictions we place on using [the subscription] in connection with
content you or another user provides, and the policies, tools and procedures we use to apply them. We
will tell you if we make a significant change to this clause.

We may remove, restrict the visibility of, or disable access to content, or restrict, suspend or

you a statement of reasons covering what we decided, why, and how to challenge it.

If you disagree with a decision we made about your content or your account, you can raise an internal

terms change takes effect and a statement of reasons on a durable medium before a restriction or
suspension. **US.** Section 230 lets this state that [trader name] is not treated as the publisher or
speaker of content you or another user supplies, and that reviewing or removing it does not change that.

## Module: your personal data

## Module: severability and entire agreement

If a court or authority finds that a part of these terms is not valid or not enforceable, the rest of

replaces the invalid part, that rule applies instead of anything stated here. These terms are the entire

**EU consumers.** A severability clause is not a drafting device that can turn one term into two; where a
