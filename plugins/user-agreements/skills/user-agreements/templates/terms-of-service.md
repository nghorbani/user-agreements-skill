# Terms of service

Law as of: 2026-10-20

A fill-in skeleton for a terms of service document covering acceptance, changes to these terms, price,
renewal and cancellation, withdrawal, warranty and liability, licensing, user content and acceptable use,
moderation, intellectual property, privacy and connected-product data, AI features, governing law and
disputes, and the standing administrative clauses. Each section is built from its own clause file in `references/clauses/`; the
bracketed modules let the same document serve an EU consumer, an EU business customer, a user in Germany
and a US user. Drop a module that does not apply to the market the document is being built for rather
than leaving it in alongside the one that does.

## How to fill this in

- Classify the contract before drafting: who the counterparty is (EU consumer, EU business customer,
  German consumer, US consumer or business), what is sold (goods, digital content supplied once, a
  continuously supplied digital service, software under licence, a hosting or user-content platform), and
  whether a price is paid in money, in personal data, or not at all. Several sections branch on this
  classification, and the wrong branch carries the wrong duties.
- Each numbered section below corresponds to one file in `references/clauses/`; where a choice is listed
  as resting on a conflicts-register entry, that entry is named in the clause file's own Drafting notes
  and decides which module to keep for a document serving more than one market.
- Fill every bracket. A bracket left in a published document is not a placeholder the reader can be
  expected to resolve; an unfilled price, address or control label defeats the point disclosure the
  underlying rule requires.
- Where a module is headed EU consumers, keep it for a consumer anywhere in the Union, Germany included;
  the Germany module adds further German-specific duties on top, it does not replace the EU one.
- Drop the US arbitration and individual-claims module entirely for an EU consumer; see the dispute
  resolution section below. Do not try to merge the two into a single clause that serves both markets at
  once.
- The five fixed-wording controls that recur across sections (the order button, the two German
  cancellation buttons, and the two German withdrawal-function controls) are checked together whenever
  any one of them changes; see the note at the end of the acceptance and formation section.
- Pull the withdrawal notice itself from `templates/withdrawal-information.md` rather than drafting it
  again here; the withdrawal section below states only when the right exists and how it works, not the
  full statutory notice text.

## 1. Acceptance of these terms

By [clicking the button labeled "[exact button label]" / continuing to [the action that completes sign-up
or checkout]], you agree to these Terms of Service and to our Privacy Policy.

[Link to the full terms, placed immediately next to this sentence and the control it names, in a color
and weight that contrasts with the surrounding text.]

Before you complete your order, you can review and change the items in your cart, your delivery address
and your payment method. Once you submit the order, we will send you an electronic confirmation to the
email address you provided.

We will keep a copy of these terms and your order details and make them available to you on request. This
agreement, your signature and your order are not denied legal effect only because they are in electronic
form.

### EU consumers

Immediately before the order button, and with nothing else between the two, give the four-point
disclosure: the technical steps to conclude the contract, whether the business will file the concluded
contract and whether the consumer can access it, the technical means to spot and correct an input error
before submitting, and the languages the contract can be concluded in (Art. 10(1)(a) to (d) Directive
2000/31/EC). Pair this with a working, not merely described, way to change the order before submission.

Make the full terms available to store and reproduce, not displayed once and gone, in every channel
including one that runs exclusively by email (Art. 10(3), 10(4) Directive 2000/31/EC). Send an electronic
acknowledgement of the order without undue delay once it is placed through the site; the order and the
acknowledgement each count as received once the addressed party can access them, not once sent (Art. 11(1)
Directive 2000/31/EC), paired with a working input-error correction step before the order is submitted
(Art. 11(2) Directive 2000/31/EC).

Keep a standing, permanently linked legal-notice page (name, geographic address, an email address for
rapid contact, trade register entry where registered, supervisory authority where one applies) separate
from this section; it is a standing duty, not a sign-up step (Art. 5(1) Directive 2000/31/EC).

### EU business customers

Art. 10(1), 10(2) and Art. 11(1), (2) Directive 2000/31/EC fall away only where the two parties have
actually agreed otherwise and neither of them is a consumer; keep the assent sentence and order-
confirmation mechanics for a business sign-up unless that actual agreement exists, and where it does,
state in the business-facing terms which of Art. 10 and 11 the parties have displaced. The storable-terms
duty and the deemed-receipt rule for when an order and its acknowledgement count as received apply even to
a contract concluded exclusively by email (Art. 10(4), Art. 11(3) Directive 2000/31/EC).

### Germany

The order button, or any other control that places a paid order, carries wording that, read on its own
with nothing else on the page supplying meaning, tells an average reasonably well informed and observant
consumer that activating it creates a payment obligation: [PW-DE-1, "zahlungspflichtig bestellen"], or an
equally unambiguous substitute (§ 312j Abs. 3 Satz 2 BGB; Case C-249/21, Fuhrmann-2-GmbH v B., judgment of
7 April 2022, paras. 26, 28 to 30, 33).

Immediately before the order button, in a highlighted manner and not behind a link, give the price, main
characteristics, delivery, duration, minimum contract term and the rest of the EGBGB Art. 246a § 1 Abs. 1
Satz 1 Nr. 1, 5 to 8, 11a, 14 and 15 information (§ 312j Abs. 2 BGB). Give the input-error correction step
in the flow itself, not through a later support channel (§ 312i Abs. 1 Nr. 1 BGB), and state, no later than
the start of the ordering process, any delivery restriction and which payment means are accepted (§ 312j
Abs. 1 BGB).

Send a confirmation on a durable medium reproducing the contract content within a reasonable time, at the
latest on delivery or before the service starts, and where we called you to conclude the contract, we
disclosed our identity and the call's commercial purpose at the start of the call (§ 312f Abs. 2 BGB;
§ 312a Abs. 1 BGB). We keep records that can later show which information you were shown and when; we
carry the burden of proving an information duty was met (§ 312m Abs. 2 BGB).

### US

Pair the assent sentence with whichever control actually completes the step it describes, and name that
control by its real, current label; update the sentence whenever the button's label changes. Place the
sentence on the same screen as the control, comparably prominent to the surrounding text, directly next
to the control, and visible without scrolling. Render a hyperlink to the terms in a color that contrasts
with the plain text around it and make it recognizable as a link. Put no unrelated question, offer or
control between the notice and the button it governs.

Where sign-up spans more than one screen, repeat the notice-and-control pairing on the one screen where
the user takes the action that forms the contract. Do not rely on a pre-checked assent box.

This agreement, a signature, and a record under it are not denied legal effect, validity or
enforceability only because they are in electronic form (15 U.S.C. § 7001(a)(1), (2)). Where a legally
required written disclosure is given electronically instead of on paper, we collect, before you consent:
a statement covering the paper option, the withdrawal right and any conditions or fees on withdrawing
consent, whether consent covers this one transaction or a category of records, the procedure to withdraw
and to update contact information, the paper-copy procedure and its fee, and a hardware-and-software
statement, followed by your electronic consent given in a way that demonstrates you can access that
electronic form (15 U.S.C. § 7001(c)(1)).

Note: the order button (above), the two German cancellation-flow buttons (section 4 below: PW-DE-2,
"Verträge hier kündigen", and PW-DE-3, "jetzt kündigen") and the German withdrawal function and its

### EU consumers

State the total price inclusive of all taxes before you are bound, or, where the exact price cannot be
calculated in advance, the method used to work it out; add every extra freight, delivery or postal charge
that applies, or say that such a charge may be payable if it cannot be calculated in advance (Art. 6(1)(e)
Directive 2011/83/EU). For a subscription, state the total price for each billing period; for a fixed-rate
contract running for an indefinite period or one that renews automatically, state the total monthly price
(Art. 6(1)(e) Directive 2011/83/EU). Where the price was set, in whole or in part, by automated
decision-making based on your behaviour, including a profile built from earlier visits, we disclose that
fact before you are bound (Art. 6(1)(ea) Directive 2011/83/EU).

We will not charge you, for using a given payment method, a fee that exceeds what accepting that method
actually costs us (Art. 19 Directive 2011/83/EU). We seek your express, separate agreement to any payment
beyond the agreed price for the main contractual obligation before you are bound; a pre-ticked option you
have to actively reject is never treated as that agreement, and you can claim back a payment charged this
way with no need to show you even noticed the charge (Art. 22 Directive 2011/83/EU). Where the contract is
concluded over a phone line we operate to deal with an already-concluded contract, we charge no more than
the basic telephone rate on that line (Art. 21 Directive 2011/83/EU).

### EU business customers

The payment-method fee cap, phone-line rate cap and express-consent-to-extra-payments duties above are
consumer protections and do not extend to a business counterparty acting in that capacity (Art. 2(1)
Directive 2011/83/EU). State the total price and payment terms in plain, intelligible language regardless.

### Germany

We make the price and the other EGBGB Art. 246a information available clearly, comprehensibly and in a
highlighted manner, immediately before the order-submission button; a link to a separate page is not
enough (§ 312j Abs. 2 BGB). We state, no later than the start of the ordering process, whether delivery
restrictions apply and which payment means are accepted (§ 312j Abs. 1 BGB). We may demand costs from you
only to the extent we informed you of those costs under EGBGB Art. 246a § 1 Abs. 1 Satz 1 Nr. 7 (§ 312e
BGB).

An extra-payment agreement beyond the main price needs our express agreement; in our checkout, such an
agreement never becomes part of the contract where it was reached through a pre-ticked default you had to
actively uncheck (§ 312a Abs. 3 BGB). A payment-method surcharge is void where a customary, reasonable,
free payment option exists alongside the surcharged one, or where the agreed fee exceeds our own cost of
that method (§ 312a Abs. 4 BGB). A premium-rate support-line charge is void where its fee exceeds the pure
telecommunications cost of the call (§ 312a Abs. 5, 6 BGB).

## 3. Changes to these terms

[Trader name] may update these terms from time to time. Where a change is more than a minor
clarification, correction of an error, or adjustment needed to keep [the service] lawful, safe or secure,
[trader name] will tell [user name] about it in advance, state the reason for the change and when it
takes effect, and give [user name] a reasonable period before the change applies.

A change does not apply to a dispute that already exists between [user name] and [trader name] before the
change takes effect. [Trader name] will not ask [user name] to accept a change to these terms through
silence or continued use alone where the change affects a right [user name] already has or a payment
[user name] already makes; where the law lets [trader name] treat continued use as acceptance, [trader
name] will still tell [user name] clearly, before the change takes effect, what continuing to use [the
service] after that date will mean, and what [user name] can do instead. If [user name] does not agree to
a change, [user name]'s option is to stop using [the service] and, where applicable, cancel the account
under section 4 (Term, renewal and cancellation), before the change takes effect.

### EU consumers

A term letting [trader name] change these terms without a valid, contract-stated reason is itself treated
as unfair and void; so is a term letting [trader name] change a characteristic of [the service] without a
valid reason, however the term is framed (Annex points (j), (k) UCTD). [Trader name] states the reason and
the method for a change in these terms themselves, not only in the notice sent once a change happens.

This clause relies on the carve-out that lets any business reserve the right to alter an indefinite-
duration contract's terms unilaterally, provided the consumer gets reasonable notice and stays free to
dissolve the contract; it still needs a valid, contract-stated reason for a change falling outside that
carve-out, and for any change to a characteristic of the service (Annex point 2(b), second sentence UCTD).

Where [the service] is digital content or a digital service supplied or kept accessible over a period, a
change to it is permitted only where all four of these hold together: these terms allow the change and
state a valid reason; the change carries no extra cost for [user name]; [user name] is told about it
clearly and comprehensibly; and, where the change does more than minimally and negatively affect [user
name]'s access to or use of [the service], [user name] is told reasonably in advance, on a durable medium,
what the change is, when it takes effect, and that [user name] may either terminate free of charge or keep
the unmodified version (Art. 19(1), (4) DCD). Where that last condition applies, [user name] gets at least
30 days from receiving the notice, or from the change taking effect, whichever is later, to exercise the
free termination right (Art. 19(2) DCD). None of this applies to a change needed to keep [the service] in
conformity, such as a security update (Art. 8(2) DCD; see section 7, Warranty and conformity).

This section does not cover a price-variation mechanism: a price change still needs its own stated reason,
its own stated method, and [user name]'s own matching right to cancel if the new price is too high, carried
in section 2 (Price and payment), since a price-variation term is never covered by the main-subject-matter
or price exclusion however transparently it is worded (Annex point (l) UCTD).

### EU business customers

The UCTD's unfairness test does not reach a contract with a business customer (Art. 2(b), (c) UCTD), and
the Digital Content Directive's Art. 19 change conditions run to consumers only (Art. 3(1) DCD). The model
wording above may be used with a business customer without the consumer-specific exit rights, but [trader
name] states the notice period and the method for change expressly in the contract.

### Germany

A clause letting [trader name] change these terms, including a price or fee change for a continuing
obligation, through [user name]'s silence or continued use, with no substantive limit on what can be
changed or by how much, unreasonably disadvantages [user name] and is invalid, even where a separate
statute also permits a deemed-consent mechanism for that kind of change and [trader name] also offers a
matching special termination right (BGH, judgment of 27 April 2021, XI ZR 26/20). This is why the model
wording above rests on actual notice and [user name]'s own choice to continue or leave, not on treating
[user name]'s inaction as acceptance of whatever [trader name] decides to change. A change clause that
fails this way is not rescued by severing it into a valid termination-right part and an invalid
change-mechanism part, since German courts treat the two as inseparable (BGH, judgment of 27 April 2021,
XI ZR 26/20).

For a recurring-supply or recurring-service continuing obligation, the renewal design in section 4 extends
the contract only onto an indefinite term and gives [user name] a contractual right to end the renewed
contract at any time on at most one month's notice; a statutory termination right [user name] separately
has stands beside this contractual right and is never replaced by it (§ 309 Nr. 9 Buchst. b BGB). Where
[trader name] reserves a release right depending on a justified, contract-stated reason, that reason is
stated in the contract itself, not only when [trader name] later relies on it (§ 308 Nr. 3 BGB). Where
[trader name] reserves a right to change or deviate from its own promised performance, the limits of that
right are stated so that, weighing [trader name]'s own interests, accepting the change still stays
reasonable for [user name] (§ 308 Nr. 4 BGB).

### US

No change to these terms applies to a dispute that has already accrued before the change takes effect, and
no change binds a person who has only browsed [the service] without transacting to a changed arbitration or
forum term (9 U.S.C. §§ 401, 402; Heckman v. Live Nation Entertainment, Inc., 120 F.4th 670 (9th Cir.
2024)). [Trader name] does not promise that a change takes effect only on explicit re-acceptance and then
apply it through mere continued use instead in an incorporated document [user name] does not see again;
that mismatch between what this section promises and what [trader name] actually does is itself evidence
of unconscionability (Heckman, 120 F.4th 670).

### EU consumers

An automatic renewal clause sets a reasonable, not unreasonably early, deadline for your objection
(UCTD Annex point (h)). Outside Germany, the EU-wide floor does not set the specific term lengths and
notice periods the Germany module below states; treat the gap between that floor and the Germany
specifics as an open point for the market the document serves (see Open points below).

### EU business customers

A business counterparty takes the German module's § 308 and § 309 content control out of play, leaving
only the general § 307 fairness test; this clause does not otherwise set a business-specific renewal or
cancellation position (see Open points below).

### Germany

Where the contract is a recurring-supply or recurring-service continuing obligation and the terms are
preformulated AGB, the renewal mechanism extends the contract onto an indefinite term, not a further fixed
term, and gives you a contractual right to end the renewed contract at any time on no more than one
month's notice; this right stands beside, not instead of, any statutory termination right you separately
have (§ 309 Nr. 9 Buchst. b BGB). Where the contract instead binds you to an initial term, a silent-renewal
clause you are bound by, or a termination notice period before the end of the initial term, the initial
term is two years or less and the pre-end notice period is one month or less, to your detriment in neither
respect; a contract for goods customarily sold together, and an insurance contract, fall outside this whole
paragraph (§ 309 Nr. 9 BGB).

Where we let you conclude this continuing paid contract through a website, we provide a Kündigungsschaltfläche,
permanently available, immediately and easily accessible, labelled [PW-DE-2, "Verträge hier kündigen"] and
nothing else, routing directly to a confirmation page labelled with the [PW-DE-3, "jetzt kündigen"]
confirmation button (§ 312k Abs. 1, 2, Abs. 2 Satz 4 BGB). The confirmation page asks for, and only for:
cancellation type and, for an extraordinary cancellation, the reason; data identifying you; data
identifying the contract; an optional desired end date you are never required to fill in; and an
electronic channel for the confirmation (§ 312k Abs. 2 Satz 3 Nr. 1 Buchst. a to e BGB). This duty binds us
even where a third-party platform runs the website the contract is concluded through.

You can store your own cancellation declaration on a durable medium, time-stamped and shown to have been
made by activating the confirmation button (§ 312k Abs. 3 BGB). We confirm receipt immediately,
electronically, in text form, stating the content, the time stamp, and the resulting end date (§ 312k
Abs. 4 BGB). Where you state no end date, the cancellation takes effect, in case of doubt, at the earliest
possible time (§ 312k Abs. 5 BGB). A missing or non-compliant button or confirmation page lets you cancel
the affected contract at any time without any notice period, whether or not a cancellation right otherwise
exists on the merits (§ 312k Abs. 6 BGB).

### US

Before taking billing information for any auto-renewing or continuous-service plan, and in visual
proximity to the control that records consent, we disclose: that the plan continues until cancelled, the
cancellation mechanism, the charge and whether and how it may change, the renewal term length or that
service is continuous, and any minimum-purchase obligation (Cal. Bus. and Prof. Code § 17601(a)(2)(A) to
(E), § 17602(a)(1); N.Y. Gen. Bus. Law § 527-a(1)(a)). Where the plan starts with a free gift, trial, or a
temporary price, we add a clear, conspicuous statement of the price that will apply once that period ends.
Where the sale is effected on the internet, we meet all three of: clear and conspicuous disclosure of the
material transaction terms before taking billing information, your express informed consent before the
first charge, and a simple mechanism to stop future charges (15 U.S.C. § 8403(1) to (3)).

Before the first charge, we obtain your affirmative consent to the agreement containing the offer terms
(Cal. Bus. and Prof. Code § 17602(a)(2), (a)(4); N.Y. Gen. Bus. Law § 527-a(1)(b)), and keep a verifiable
record of that consent for at least three years, or one year after the contract ends, whichever is longer
(Cal. Bus. and Prof. Code § 17602(a)(6)). After consent, we send a retainable acknowledgment covering the
offer terms, the cancellation policy, and how to cancel (Cal. Bus. and Prof. Code § 17602(a)(3); N.Y. Gen.
Bus. Law § 527-a(1)(c)(i) to (v)).

We describe our cancellation mechanism: [a toll-free telephone number / an email address / a postal
address / another cost-effective, timely and easy-to-use method] (Cal. Bus. and Prof. Code
§ 17602(c)(1)), reachable through at least the same medium you used to consent (Cal. Bus. and Prof. Code
§ 17602(f); N.Y. Gen. Bus. Law § 527-a(1)(d), (d-1)). Where online acceptance is offered, we add no
further step beyond permitted account authentication that obstructs or delays immediate online termination
(Cal. Bus. and Prof. Code § 17602(d)(1), (d)(2)).

Where a material change, including a fee change, is made to accepted terms, we give clear, conspicuous,
retainable notice with cancellation instructions before the change takes effect (Cal. Bus. and Prof. Code
§ 17602(g)(1), (g)(2); N.Y. Gen. Bus. Law § 527-a(1)(g)). In New York, on a price increase, or on a price
higher than what was disclosed at the offer stage, we either capture your affirmative consent to the new
price before charging it, or let you cancel at any time within at least fourteen days after the charge,
with a pro rata refund for the remaining term as of the cancellation date (N.Y. Gen. Bus. Law
§ 527-a(1)(b-1)).

We do not condition your ability to cancel on hearing a retention offer first, and an unconsented shipment
under a continuous service or automatic renewal is treated as an unconditional gift, with no invoice,
collection, or return-shipping charge against you (Cal. Bus. and Prof. Code § 17603; N.Y. Gen. Bus. Law
§ 527-a(2)).

### EU consumers

A mixed order keeps one withdrawal period for the whole contract, set by its main subject matter, not a
separate period per item (Art. 9(2) Directive 2011/83/EU). Where the trader concludes the contract through
an online interface, this duty applies on top of the letter-or-email withdrawal route, not instead of it,
and reaches a third-party platform the trader sells through as much as the trader's own site (Art. 11a(1)
Directive 2011/83/EU).

Where this contract has an ancillary contract attached to it, withdrawing from the main contract
automatically ends the ancillary contract too, at no cost to the consumer, except that a linked credit
agreement follows its own Article 15 regime under the Consumer Credit Directive instead (Art. 15(1)
Directive 2011/83/EU).

Where the trader omits the Art. 6(1)(h) withdrawal-right information, the 14-day period extends to 12
months from the end of the original period, then expires 14 (or 30) days after the consumer is later given
that information (Art. 10(1), (2) Directive 2011/83/EU).

Name every withdrawal exception this contract actually relies on, drawn only from the closed Art. 16 list
(goods made to the consumer's specification; sealed goods unsuitable for return for health or hygiene
reasons once unsealed; goods that mix inseparably with other items after delivery; sealed audio, video or
software once unsealed; a subscription to a newspaper, periodical or magazine outside a subscription
agreement for regular delivery; digital content on a non-tangible medium already supplied with the
consumer's express prior consent and acknowledgment of losing the right; and the other closed-list cases a
given contract may trigger). State the exception by its own facts, never by its label alone (Art. 16
Directive 2011/83/EU).

### EU business customers

Drop this section for a contract with a business customer acting in that capacity; the withdrawal right is
a consumer protection and does not reach a trade or professional counterparty (Art. 2(1) Directive
2011/83/EU).

### Germany

The two labelled controls for the online withdrawal function are worded exactly as the statute gives them,
or an equally unambiguous equivalent: PW-DE-4 for the withdrawal function itself, "Vertrag widerrufen", and
PW-DE-5 for its confirmation function, "Widerruf bestätigen" (§ 356a Abs. 1 to 3 BGB). Keep both controls
permanently available, reachable from every subpage, and not gated behind a login or an app install the
contract itself did not require.

The withdrawal period's start follows the specific rule the contract type triggers: receipt of the goods,
or of the last good, lot or first regular delivery, for a goods purchase; conclusion, for water, gas or
electricity supplied without a fixed volume or quantity, for district heating, and for non-tangible
digital content (§ 356 Abs. 2 BGB). The period does not start at all until the gating EGBGB information has
actually been given (§ 356 Abs. 3 BGB); where that information was omitted, the same 12-month-plus long-
stop in the EU consumers module applies here (§ 356 Abs. 4 BGB).

We state the restitution deadline as 14 days after withdrawal for goods and ordinary services, extended to
30 days for a financial-service contract (§ 357 Abs. 1 BGB, § 357b Abs. 1 BGB), and we never charge value
replacement for withdrawing from non-physical digital content (§ 357a Abs. 3 BGB).

### US

These references cover no US withdrawal or cooling-off right, so this section carries no US module. Do
not reuse the EU 14-day period, the withdrawal function, or the model form language for a US-facing
document on this basis; a US cancellation or refund right, where one applies, rests on separate grounds
this section does not state (see Open points below).
