# Implementation requirements

Law as of: 2026-10-20

This is the hand-over register from the legal references to the product and engineering teams that build the checkout, account and data flows. Each row names one thing the product must do, the rule it rests on, who owns building and checking it, what evidence proves it is done, and its status. Fill in Owner and Status for your own team; Requirement, Legal basis and Evidence come from the reference files and do not change without a reference-file change.

Status values: not started, in progress, built, verified.
