# Impressum

Law as of: 2026-10-20

A fill-in template for the German legal notice (Impressum) and the accompanying dispute-resolution
notice, built on § 5 DDG and §§ 36 to 37 VSBG.

## How to fill this in

- Work through the core list first, then the conditional list, against the provider's actual legal
  form, licensing status, registration and business. The applicable items form a closed set: dropping
  one that applies is not cured by including the rest, and including an item that does not apply adds
  nothing (§ 5 Abs. 1 Nr. 1 to 8 DDG).
- Core items, every provider of a commercial digital service: name and establishment address; for a
  legal person, also its legal form, its authorized representative, and, only where capital figures
  are stated at all, the share or nominal capital and, if not all cash contributions are paid in, the
  total outstanding contributions (§ 5 Abs. 1 Nr. 1 DDG). Add fast-contact details including an email
  address (§ 5 Abs. 1 Nr. 2 DDG).
- Conditional items, include only where the condition holds for this provider:
  - the service needs official authorization: name the competent supervisory authority (§ 5 Abs. 1
    Nr. 3 DDG).
  - the provider is entered in a commercial register or similar register: name the register and the
    registration number (§ 5 Abs. 1 Nr. 4 DDG).
  - the provider holds a VAT identification number under § 27a Abs. 1 Satz 1, 2 or 3
    Umsatzsteuergesetz, or an Economic ID under § 139c Abs. 1 Abgabenordnung: state it (§ 5 Abs. 1
    Nr. 6 DDG).
  - the provider pursues a regulated profession within the meaning of Art. 1 Buchst. d of Richtlinie
    89/48/EWG or Art. 1 Buchst. f of Richtlinie 92/51/EWG (as last amended by Richtlinie
    2006/100/EG): name the professional chamber the provider belongs to, the statutory professional
    title and the state where it was conferred, and the professional-conduct rules and how to access
    them (§ 5 Abs. 1 Nr. 5 Buchst. a to c DDG).
  - the provider is an Aktiengesellschaft, Kommanditgesellschaft auf Aktien or Gesellschaft mit
    beschränkter Haftung in winding-up or liquidation: state that fact (§ 5 Abs. 1 Nr. 7 DDG).
  - the provider is an audiovisual media service provider: name the member state that is, or is
    deemed to be, its seat state, and the competent regulatory and supervisory authorities (§ 5 Abs. 1
    Nr. 8 Buchst. a, b DDG).
- This page itself, or a page linked clearly from every entry point of the service, is the single
  place that carries the full list: do not split the required items across several pages with no
  clear link between them, and do not place them behind unrelated content (§ 5 Abs. 1 DDG chapeau).
  The source sets no fixed click count or layout rule for how directly this page must be reached from
  elsewhere in the service; see Open points to raise.
- § 5 Abs. 2 DDG leaves other statutory information duties in place alongside this one; this template
  does not state them, and a provider subject to a sector-specific duty (for example under
  professional or financial-services law) still owes it separately.
- Add the dispute-resolution block below once for each location it applies to: whether the business
  maintains a website, uses standard terms toward consumers, or both. If the business uses standard
  terms, deliver the same information together with those terms, in addition to the website where it
  maintains one as well (§ 36 Abs. 2 VSBG).
- Fill the willingness statement truthfully for this business's actual position: either it is willing
  or obliged to take part in dispute-resolution proceedings before a named consumer arbitration body,
  and then name that body with its address and website; or it is not willing and not obliged, and
  says so (§ 36 Abs. 1 Nr. 1, Nr. 2, Abs. 2 Nr. 1 VSBG).
- A business with 10 or fewer employees on the prior 31 December is exempt from the general
  willingness statement (§ 36 Abs. 3 VSBG), but the exemption does not reach a case where the business
  has in fact committed, or is legally obliged, to take part before a named body: an exempt business
  in that position still names the body, its address and its website. Check headcount every year;
  the exemption can lapse or newly apply as headcount changes.
- Do not include any link to the EU Online Dispute Resolution platform or any citation of Regulation
  (EU) No 524/2013 as a live channel: the platform stopped taking new complaints on 20 March 2025 and
  its legal basis was repealed with effect from 20 July 2025 (Art. 1, Art. 2(1), 2(2) Regulation (EU)
  2024/3228). If the business wants to point consumers to an out-of-court option at all, see Open
  points to raise for the only replacement reference point this source gives.
- The § 37 VSBG notice is a separate, later step, not covered by this page: it fires only once an
  actual dispute with a specific consumer has failed to settle, must be delivered in text form, and
  names the competent consumer arbitration body for that consumer together with the business's
  willingness or obligation position (§ 37 Abs. 1, Abs. 2 VSBG). Do not try to pre-fill it here or
  treat this page's standing notice as covering it.
- Check every citation in this template against the cut legal text before relying on it; the
  citations above were checked at the time this template was written.

## Module: natural person or sole trader (no legal person)

Use this module where the provider is a natural person or a sole trader, not a company or other legal
person.

```
Impressum

Angaben gemäß § 5 DDG

[full name of the provider]
[street and house number]
[postal code and city]
[country, if outside Germany]

Kontakt

E-Mail: [email address]
[second contact channel, for example telephone number]

[Where applicable: Umsatzsteuer-Identifikationsnummer gemäß § 27a Umsatzsteuergesetz: [VAT ID]]
[Where applicable: Wirtschafts-Identifikationsnummer gemäß § 139c Abgabenordnung: [Economic ID]]
[Where applicable: Zuständige Aufsichtsbehörde: [name and address of the supervisory authority]]
[Where applicable: Eingetragen im [register name] des [court or body], Registernummer: [number]]
[Where applicable, regulated profession: Kammer: [chamber name]; verliehen in: [state of conferral];
Berufsbezeichnung: [professional title]; es gelten folgende berufsrechtliche Regelungen:
[name of the rules], einsehbar unter: [where to access them]]
[Where the provider is an audiovisual media service provider: Mitgliedstaat, der als Sitzstaat gilt:
[member state]. Zuständige Regulierungs- und Aufsichtsbehörden: [name and contact of the
authorities].]
```

## Module: legal person (company)

Use this module where the provider is a legal person (for example a GmbH, AG, KGaA, UG, or a
registered association).

```
Impressum

Angaben gemäß § 5 DDG

[company name]
[legal form, if not already in the name]
[street and house number]
[postal code and city]
[country, if outside Germany]

Vertreten durch: [name of the authorized representative, for example the managing director]

Kontakt

E-Mail: [email address]
[second contact channel, for example telephone number]

[Where applicable: Eingetragen im [register name, for example Handelsregister] des
[court], Registernummer: [number]]
[Where applicable, and only where capital figures are stated at all: Stamm- oder Grundkapital:
[amount]. [Where not all cash contributions are paid in, add:] Gesamtbetrag der ausstehenden
Einlagen: [amount]]
[Where applicable: Umsatzsteuer-Identifikationsnummer gemäß § 27a Umsatzsteuergesetz: [VAT ID]]
[Where applicable: Wirtschafts-Identifikationsnummer gemäß § 139c Abgabenordnung: [Economic ID]]
[Where applicable: Zuständige Aufsichtsbehörde: [name and address of the supervisory authority]]
[Where applicable, regulated profession: Kammer: [chamber name]; verliehen in: [state of conferral];
Berufsbezeichnung: [professional title]; es gelten folgende berufsrechtliche Regelungen:
[name of the rules], einsehbar unter: [where to access them]]
[Where the company is in winding-up or liquidation and is an Aktiengesellschaft, Kommanditgesellschaft
auf Aktien or Gesellschaft mit beschränkter Haftung: Die Gesellschaft befindet sich in Abwicklung /
Liquidation.]
[Where the provider is an audiovisual media service provider: Mitgliedstaat, der als Sitzstaat gilt:
[member state]. Zuständige Regulierungs- und Aufsichtsbehörden: [name and contact of the
authorities].]
```

## Module: dispute resolution notice (§§ 36 to 37 VSBG)

Place this block on the website where the business maintains one, and again together with the
business's standard terms where it uses them, so each location it applies to carries it
independently (§ 36 Abs. 2 VSBG).

```
Verbraucherstreitbeilegung

[Where the business is willing or legally obliged to take part:] Wir sind bereit [or: verpflichtet],
an einem Streitbeilegungsverfahren vor einer Verbraucherschlichtungsstelle teilzunehmen. Zuständige
Verbraucherschlichtungsstelle: [name of the consumer arbitration body], [address], [website].

[Where the business is neither willing nor obliged, and not within the § 36 Abs. 3 exemption, or
wants to state its position plainly:] Wir sind nicht bereit und nicht verpflichtet, an einem
Streitbeilegungsverfahren vor einer Verbraucherschlichtungsstelle teilzunehmen.
```

Variant note: an exempt business (10 or fewer employees on the prior 31 December, § 36 Abs. 3 VSBG)
may leave out the general willingness statement, but must still use the first variant, naming the
body, if it has in fact committed, or is legally obliged, to take part before one.

## How to fill in the § 37 notice (separate from this page)

Use this only once an actual dispute with a specific consumer has failed to settle; deliver it in
text form to that consumer, not as part of the standing page above.

```
Der Streit zwischen Ihnen und uns über den oben genannten Vertrag konnte nicht einvernehmlich
beigelegt werden. Zuständige Verbraucherschlichtungsstelle: [name of the competent consumer
arbitration body for this consumer], [address], [website]. [State the willingness or obligation
position:] Wir sind bereit [or: verpflichtet] [or: nicht bereit und nicht verpflichtet], an einem
Streitbeilegungsverfahren vor dieser Stelle teilzunehmen.
```

## Open points to raise

- Whether the provider's actual legal form, licensing status, register entry, profession, VAT or
  Economic ID holding, winding-up status and audiovisual-media status have been checked item by item
  against the § 5 Abs. 1 list, since a template filled from a generic pattern risks omitting an item
  that applies to this specific provider, or including one that does not (§ 5 Abs. 1 Nr. 1 to 8 DDG).
- Whether this page's placement meets unmittelbar erreichbar in practice across every entry point of
  the service, since the source states the standard without a numeric click count or layout rule (§ 5
  Abs. 1 DDG chapeau).
- Whether the business wants to keep pointing consumers to an out-of-court dispute-resolution option
  at all once any ODR-platform link is removed, and if so how it wants to frame a reference to the
  Commission's list of alternative dispute resolution entities kept under Article 20(4) of Directive
  2013/11/EU, the Union-level replacement reference point; this source does not fix wording for a
  trader's own document (Art. 4 Regulation (EU) 2024/3228).
- Whether any clause in the business's own documents still carries a link to the ODR platform or a
  citation of Regulation (EU) No 524/2013, since both should already be removed given that the
  platform stopped taking complaints on 20 March 2025 and its legal basis was repealed on 20 July
  2025 (Art. 1, Art. 2(1), 2(2) Regulation (EU) 2024/3228).
- Whether the business's employee headcount on the prior 31 December has been checked this year
  against the § 36 Abs. 3 VSBG exemption threshold, since the exemption can lapse or newly apply as
  headcount changes.
