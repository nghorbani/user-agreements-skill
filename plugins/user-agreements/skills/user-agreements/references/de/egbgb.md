# EGBGB information duties and model forms

Citation: Einführungsgesetz zum Bürgerlichen Gesetzbuche (EGBGB), Art. 246 to 246e, Anlage 1 and 2
Version: Neugefasst durch Bek. v. 21.9.1994 I 2494; 1997, 1061; zuletzt geändert durch Art. 2 G v. 16.7.2026 I Nr. 212
Official text: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgbeg/
Law as of: 2026-10-20

This file covers what EGBGB Art. 246, 246a, 246c, 246d and 246e require of German consumer terms and checkouts. The model forms themselves, Anlage 1 (Muster-Widerrufsbelehrung, model withdrawal information) and Anlage 2 (Muster-Widerrufsformular, model withdrawal form), are not reproduced here; see PW-DE-6 and PW-DE-7 in `references/prescribed-wording.md` for their full text. Each EGBGB article serves a specific BGB anchor provision: Art. 246 serves § 312a BGB, Art. 246a serves § 312d Abs. 1 BGB, Art. 246c serves § 312i BGB, and Art. 246d stands alone for online-marketplace operators; see `references/de/bgb-fernabsatz.md` for those BGB provisions.

## When it applies

- A trader (Unternehmer, as defined for the BGB) owes the consumer (Verbraucher) the Art. 246 Abs. 1 information before the consumer's contractual declaration, unless the information is already obvious from the circumstances (Art. 246 Abs. 1 EGBGB, serving § 312a Abs. 2 BGB). This baseline does not apply to everyday transactions performed immediately at conclusion (Art. 246 Abs. 2 EGBGB).
- The fuller Art. 246a § 1 duty list applies to off-premises and distance contracts other than financial services (Art. 246a § 1 Abs. 1 EGBGB, serving § 312d Abs. 1 BGB); Art. 246b applies instead for financial services (serving § 312d Abs. 2 BGB); this file does not cover Art. 246b, since the skill's typical SaaS and app products sit outside financial services, but flag any financial-services product as an open point.
- A reduced disclosure set applies to an off-premises repair or maintenance contract the consumer expressly requested, performed immediately on both sides, with consideration up to EUR 200: only trader identity and contact, plus price or pricing method with a total-cost estimate, are owed at the point of service; the full list still applies to any later written confirmation (Art. 246a § 2 EGBGB).
- A reduced core list applies where a distance contract is concluded via a medium with only limited space or time for information, such as SMS or a voice-only channel: main characteristics, trader identity, total price, withdrawal conditions, and contract duration or termination terms, with the rest still owed in a suitable way (Art. 246a § 3 EGBGB).
- E-commerce procedural disclosure under Art. 246c applies to any contract concluded in electronic business transactions, serving § 312i BGB.
- Art. 246d applies to the operator of an online marketplace, a role distinct from the third-party trader selling on it.

## What the documents must say

- Give the Art. 246 Abs. 1 Nr. 1 to 5d list for a consumer contract generally: main characteristics; trader identity, address and phone; total price or pricing method with conditional shipping and other costs; payment, delivery and performance terms with a delivery date and complaints procedure; the statutory warranty notice using the harmonised EU notice; a manufacturer durability-guarantee notice where offered free and beyond two years, using the harmonised EU label; the digital-content or digital-service statutory warranty reminder; customer-service and commercial-guarantee terms; the minimum software-update period where disclosed; contract duration or termination terms; functionality; compatibility and interoperability where material and known; the repairability score, or, where no score applies, spare-parts and repair-manual availability (Art. 246 Abs. 1 Nr. 1 to 5, 5a to 5d, 6 to 10 EGBGB).
- Where a withdrawal right exists, give the consumer a Widerrufsbelehrung (withdrawal information) in Textform containing, at minimum: notice of the right to withdraw; notice that withdrawal requires no justification; the name and a service-of-process-capable address of whoever withdrawal must be declared to; and the withdrawal period's length and start, with notice that timely dispatch suffices (Art. 246 Abs. 3 EGBGB). This four-element floor is the minimum; the full off-premises and distance version is Art. 246a § 1 Abs. 2, built out in full by Anlage 1 (PW-DE-6).
- For off-premises and distance contracts other than financial services, give the Art. 246a § 1 Abs. 1 Nr. 1 to 11c, 12 to 21 list: the Art. 246 items plus a principal's identity where the trader acts for one; a business address distinct from the contact address for complaints; personalised-pricing notice where an automated decision set the price; distance-communication-tool cost notice; the subscription or open-ended-contract total-price-per-period disclosure; the minimum consumer-commitment duration; a security-deposit or financial-guarantee notice; and out-of-court dispute-resolution access (Art. 246a § 1 Abs. 1 EGBGB). Where the contract is concluded at a publicly accessible auction, the auctioneer's corresponding details may stand in for the trader-identity items (Art. 246a § 1 Abs. 1 Satz 2 EGBGB).
- Where a § 312g Abs. 1 BGB withdrawal right exists, inform the consumer on withdrawal conditions, period and procedure under § 355 Abs. 1 BGB, offer the Anlage 2 model form (PW-DE-7), and, where applicable, state the existence and placement of the electronic withdrawal function under § 356a BGB; state return-cost allocation including for goods that cannot normally be returned by post; and state the proportional-payment duty under § 357a Abs. 2 BGB where the consumer asked for early performance (Art. 246a § 1 Abs. 2 Satz 1 Nr. 1 to 3 EGBGB).
- Where no withdrawal right exists under the § 312g Abs. 2 BGB exceptions, or where a withdrawal right that initially existed can lapse early, inform the consumer of that fact and of the circumstances under which it lapses (Art. 246a § 1 Abs. 3 EGBGB).
- For e-commerce, inform the customer, serving § 312i BGB: the individual technical steps leading to contract conclusion; whether the contract text is stored post-conclusion and accessible to the customer; how input errors can be detected and corrected before the contractual declaration; the languages available for concluding the contract; and all codes of conduct the trader subscribes to, with how to access them electronically (Art. 246c Nr. 1 to 5 EGBGB).
- As an operator of an online marketplace, inform the consumer on: the main ranking parameters for search results and their relative weighting; which providers were included in building any comparison result shown; whether the operator and the offeror are affiliated companies; whether the offeror is a trader per the offeror's own declaration, and, if not, that the special consumer-contract rules do not apply to that contract; the extent to which the offeror relies on the operator to fulfil contractual obligations toward the consumer, and that this creates no direct claim against the operator; and, for resold event-admission rights, whether and at what price the organiser originally set the admission right (Art. 246d § 1 Nr. 1 to 7 EGBGB). Give all of it before the consumer's contract declaration, clearly and comprehensibly (Art. 246d § 2 Abs. 1 EGBGB), and the ranking and comparison items (Nr. 1 and 2) in a defined area of the interface that is directly and easily accessible from the page showing the offers (Art. 246d § 2 Abs. 2 EGBGB).

## What the documents must not say

- No terms document may declare the withdrawal-information duty satisfied by a non-compliant or incomplete model, since an incorrectly or incompletely filled-in Anlage 1 does not discharge Art. 246a § 1 Abs. 2 Satz 1 Nr. 1 to 3; the general duty and its own full content then apply instead.
- No checkout or notice may bury the Art. 246a § 1 information behind a separate terms page reached only by a link, in place of giving it directly in the flow; this file states the content duty, and `references/de/bgb-fernabsatz.md` states the related placement and order-button rules at § 312j BGB.
- No marketplace operator may put its ranking-parameter disclosure only in a general terms-of-service document; placement outside the defined, directly accessible interface area does not meet Art. 246d § 2 Abs. 2 EGBGB, even if the content itself is complete.
- No clause can cure, after the fact, a missing disclosure, a missing confirmation, or a missing withdrawal function; the Art. 246e § 1 Abs. 2 catalog treats these as factual or procedural failures, not clause-drafting defects a better clause could retroactively fix.

## Product behaviour it assumes

- The withdrawal-information flow uses Anlage 1 (PW-DE-6), correctly completed for the contract type and sent in Textform, since that is the only path with a statutory discharge clause (Art. 246a § 1 Abs. 2 Satz 2 EGBGB); Anlage 2 (PW-DE-7) is offered alongside it, but its use by the consumer is never required.
- Where the contract is concluded via an online interface, the Anlage 1 online-function paragraph is inserted with the actual access point, and the § 356a BGB receipt confirmation is sent on activation; omitting either where the trader does operate such a function is independently sanctionable (Art. 246e § 1 Abs. 2 Nr. 14a EGBGB).
- An online marketplace shows its ranking and comparison information in a defined area of the interface that the page with the offers leads to directly, and the other Art. 246d § 1 information before the consumer's contract declaration (Art. 246d § 2 Abs. 1, 2 EGBGB).
- Records exist to reconstruct, after the fact, which Art. 246 to 246d information was shown to a given consumer and when, since the trader bears that burden; in the reduced-disclosure EUR 200 repair call-out, any written copy or confirmation of the contract still carries the complete Art. 246a § 1 list (Art. 246a § 2 Abs. 3 EGBGB). The limited-space-medium case has no equivalent full-confirmation duty: the rest of the Art. 246a § 1 list only needs to be made accessible in a suitable way, subject to Art. 246a § 4 Abs. 3 (Art. 246a § 3 Satz 2 EGBGB).

## Prescribed wording

- PW-DE-6: Muster-Widerrufsbelehrung, EGBGB Anlage 1; goes into the withdrawal-information section of the terms, order confirmation, or a dedicated withdrawal-policy page for any off-premises or distance consumer contract outside financial services where a withdrawal right exists, completed per its own Gestaltungshinweise and sent in Textform.
- PW-DE-7: Muster-Widerrufsformular, EGBGB Anlage 2; goes alongside PW-DE-6 as the form offered to the consumer, with the trader's own name, address and email address inserted in its first line.
- This file does not introduce PW-DE-1 to PW-DE-5 (the order-button and cancellation-button wording and the § 356a BGB function labels): those are BGB-level duties covered in `references/de/bgb-fernabsatz.md` and `references/de/bgb-widerruf.md`.

## Dates

- The harmonised statutory-warranty notice and durability-guarantee label referenced at Art. 246 Abs. 1 Nr. 5, 5a and Art. 246a § 1 Abs. 1 Nr. 11, 11a EGBGB follow Durchführungsverordnung (EU) 2025/1960 in der Fassung vom 25. September 2025; that regulation's own annexes set the notice and label design, outside the texts covered here, and should be checked directly before drafting either disclosure.
- The Art. 246e § 2 Abs. 5 enforcement gate cross-refers to Verordnung (EU) 2017/2394 in der Fassung vom 19. Dezember 2024; a fine under this chapter can only be imposed within a coordinated enforcement action under that regulation's Art. 21, not as an ordinary single-case fine.
- 2026-10-20: law-as-of date for this file.

## Open points to raise

- Whether the seller's product includes a distance financial-services offering, since that shifts the applicable information list from Art. 246a to Art. 246b, which this file does not cover.
- Whether the business can reconstruct, after the fact, what information was shown to a specific consumer and when, since the trader bears the burden of proving an information duty was met.
- Whether the seller's online interface is itself an Online-Marktplatz under the BGB's marketplace definition, which would add the Art. 246d duties to the seller's own terms obligations.
- Whether any contract genuinely forms through an online interface, which determines whether the § 356a BGB electronic withdrawal function, and its Anlage 1 cross-reference, must be built at all.
- Whether a given off-premises repair or maintenance scenario in the product's business model falls within the Art. 246a § 2 reduced-disclosure case, since the EUR 200 and immediate-performance conditions are strict.
