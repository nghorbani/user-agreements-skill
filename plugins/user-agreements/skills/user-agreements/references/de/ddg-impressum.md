# DDG: Impressum and commercial communications

Citation: Digitale-Dienste-Gesetz (DDG), §§ 5, 6 and 11
Version: zuletzt geändert durch Art. 26 Abs. 2 G v. 25.3.2026 I Nr. 81
Official text: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ddg/
Law as of: 2026-10-20

The DDG replaces the former Telemediengesetz and carries Germany's accompanying rules to the EU
Digital Services Act. § 5 sets the Impressum (legal notice) duty: a fixed, closed list of identity
and contact information every commercial digital-service provider must keep permanently available.
§ 6 sets labeling and identifiability rules for commercial communications, including a header
integrity rule for commercial email and marking duties for video-sharing platforms. § 11 requires
a video-sharing platform to secure an effective contractual ban on its users posting audiovisual
commercial communication that advertises tobacco or medicinal products in violation of the named
advertising statutes.

## When it applies

- § 5 Abs. 1 applies to a Diensteanbieter (service provider) of a digital service offered
  geschäftsmäßig (on a commercial, business basis), in der Regel gegen Entgelt (normally for
  payment); a purely private, non-commercial offering falls outside it (§ 5 Abs. 1 DDG chapeau).
- § 5 Abs. 1's eight items apply conditionally, not as one fixed block: Nr. 1 applies to every
  provider, with extra items only where the provider is a legal person; Nr. 3 only where the
  service is offered or provided in the course of an activity needing official authorization;
  Nr. 4 only where the provider is entered in the commercial register or a similar register; Nr. 5
  only where the service is offered or provided in exercise of a profession within the meaning of
  Art. 1 Buchst. d of Richtlinie 89/48/EWG or Art. 1 Buchst. f of Richtlinie 92/51/EWG (as last
  amended by Richtlinie 2006/100/EG); Nr. 6 only where the provider holds a VAT identification
  number under § 27a Abs. 1 Satz 1, 2 or 3 Umsatzsteuergesetz or an Economic ID under § 139c Abs. 1
  Abgabenordnung; Nr. 7 only for an Aktiengesellschaft, Kommanditgesellschaft auf Aktien or
  Gesellschaft mit beschränkter Haftung in winding-up or liquidation; Nr. 8 only for a provider of
  audiovisual media services (§ 5 Abs. 1 Nr. 1 to 8 DDG).
- § 6 Abs. 1 applies to kommerzielle Kommunikationen (commercial communications) that are digital
  services or part of a digital service; this section does not itself define the term. Its four
  duties are a mindestens (at minimum) standard, so a stricter rule elsewhere is not displaced by
  meeting this floor (§ 6 Abs. 1 DDG chapeau).
- § 6 Abs. 2 applies specifically where a commercial communication is sent by electronic mail
  (§ 6 Abs. 2 Satz 1 DDG).
- § 6 Abs. 3 and 4 apply specifically to a Videosharingplattform-Anbieter (video-sharing platform
  provider), for user-generated videos uploaded to that service (§ 6 Abs. 3, 4 DDG), and only once
  the upload meets the § 1 Abs. 4 Nr. 7 definition of audiovisuelle kommerzielle Kommunikation: image
  communication (with or without sound) attached to or contained in a Sendung or a nutzergeneriertes
  Video, made for payment, for a similar consideration, or as Eigenwerbung (self-promotion), that
  serves the direct or indirect promotion of the sale of goods or services, or of the image of a
  natural or legal person pursuing an economic activity (§ 1 Abs. 4 Nr. 7 DDG). A video outside that
  definition triggers neither the Abs. 3 declaration function nor the Abs. 4 marking duty.
- § 11 Abs. 1 applies to a Videosharingplattform-Anbieter and requires it to agree effectively with
  its users that they may not distribute unzulässige audiovisuelle kommerzielle Kommunikation
  (impermissible audiovisual commercial communication) on the platform; § 11 Abs. 2 defines that term
  narrowly, by reference to two other statutes, not as a general advertising-content filter (§ 11
  Abs. 1, 2 DDG). The underlying term audiovisuelle kommerzielle Kommunikation carries the same § 1
  Abs. 4 Nr. 7 definition as above, so the contractual ban reaches only uploads that meet it (§ 1
  Abs. 4 Nr. 7 DDG).
- § 5 Abs. 2 and § 6 Abs. 5 both state that other legal regimes continue to apply alongside this
  file: further information duties under other statutes are unaffected by § 5 (§ 5 Abs. 2 DDG), and
  the Gesetz gegen den unlauteren Wettbewerb (UWG) and the Preisangabenverordnung (PAngV) are
  unaffected by § 6 (§ 6 Abs. 5 DDG). This file does not cover either of those regimes.

## What the documents must say

- An Impressum page (or equivalent legal notice) states every item from § 5 Abs. 1 Nr. 1 to 8 that
  applies to the provider's actual legal form, licensing status, and business, kept ständig
  verfügbar (permanently available), leicht erkennbar (easily recognizable) and unmittelbar
  erreichbar (directly accessible) (§ 5 Abs. 1 DDG chapeau). Nr. 1's core is name and address of
  establishment for every provider; a legal person adds its legal form, its authorized
  representative, and, where capital figures are stated at all, the share or nominal capital and,
  if not all cash contributions are paid in, the total outstanding contributions (§ 5 Abs. 1 Nr. 1
  DDG).
- Nr. 2: details enabling fast electronic contact and direct communication, including an address
  for electronic mail (§ 5 Abs. 1 Nr. 2 DDG).
- Nr. 3: where the service is offered or provided in an activity needing official authorization,
  the competent supervisory authority (§ 5 Abs. 1 Nr. 3 DDG).
- Nr. 4: the commercial register or similar register the provider is entered in, and the matching
  registration number (§ 5 Abs. 1 Nr. 4 DDG).
- Nr. 5: for a regulated profession within the directives named above, three further items: the
  Kammer (professional chamber) the provider belongs to (Buchst. a); the statutory professional
  title and the state where it was conferred (Buchst. b); and the name of the applicable
  professional-conduct rules and how to access them (Buchst. c) (§ 5 Abs. 1 Nr. 5 Buchst. a to c
  DDG).
- Nr. 6: where the provider holds a VAT identification number or an Economic ID, that number
  (§ 5 Abs. 1 Nr. 6 DDG).
- Nr. 7: for an Aktiengesellschaft, Kommanditgesellschaft auf Aktien, or Gesellschaft mit
  beschränkter Haftung in winding-up or liquidation, a statement to that effect (§ 5 Abs. 1 Nr. 7
  DDG).
- Nr. 8: for an audiovisual media service provider, the member state that is, or is deemed to be,
  its seat state, and the competent regulatory and supervisory authorities (§ 5 Abs. 1 Nr. 8 Buchst.
  a, b DDG).
- A terms document may link to a separate Impressum page rather than repeating every item itself,
  as long as that page independently meets the leicht erkennbar and unmittelbar erreichbar standard
  (§ 5 Abs. 1 DDG chapeau). The source does not state a layout rule for where that link must sit; see
  Open points to raise.
- For commercial communications, label the communication itself so it is clearly recognizable as
  commercial (§ 6 Abs. 1 Nr. 1 DDG), and make the natural or legal person on whose behalf it is made
  clearly identifiable (§ 6 Abs. 1 Nr. 2 DDG).
- For a sales promotion (price reduction, premium, or gift), mark the offer clearly as such, and
  state the conditions for claiming it so they are easily accessible and clear and unambiguous
  (§ 6 Abs. 1 Nr. 3 DDG).
- For a prize draw or advertising-character competition, mark it clearly as such, and state the
  participation conditions so they are easily accessible and clear and unambiguous (§ 6 Abs. 1 Nr. 4
  DDG).
- For commercial communications sent by email, keep the header and subject line free of anything
  that disguises or conceals either the sender or the commercial character of the message
  (§ 6 Abs. 2 Satz 1 DDG).
- A video-sharing platform provider offers uploaders of user-generated video a function letting
  them declare whether their video contains audiovisual commercial communication (§ 6 Abs. 3 DDG),
  and marks audiovisual commercial communication uploaded by users once the provider has learned of
  it, whether through that declaration or otherwise (§ 6 Abs. 4 DDG).
- A video-sharing platform's terms with its users effectively agree a ban on distributing
  impermissible audiovisual commercial communication on the platform, where impermissible means
  audiovisual commercial communication that violates § 20 Tabakerzeugnisgesetz (tobacco-product
  advertising) or § 10 Heilmittelwerbegesetz (medicinal-product advertising) (§ 11 Abs. 1, 2 DDG).

## What the documents must not say

- A document cannot assert that an Impressum satisfies § 5 Abs. 1 by listing only some of the items
  that apply to the provider's actual legal form and licensing status; the applicable items from
  Nr. 1 to 8 form a closed, conditional list, and dropping one that applies is not cured by
  including the rest (§ 5 Abs. 1 Nr. 1 to 8 DDG).
- A document cannot claim the leicht erkennbar and unmittelbar erreichbar standard is met by
  splitting the required items across several pages with no clear link between them, or by placing
  them behind unrelated content, even where each individual item is technically present somewhere
  on the service (§ 5 Abs. 1 DDG chapeau).
- A document cannot assert that a commercial communication needs no labeling because it also
  appears, elsewhere, in a general terms-of-service document; § 6 Abs. 1's four duties attach to
  the communication itself, not to a disclosure buried in unrelated terms (§ 6 Abs. 1 Nr. 1 to 4
  DDG).
- A document cannot assert that an email header or subject line is compliant merely because it is
  literally true; § 6 Abs. 2 Satz 2 judges a disguised or concealed sender or commercial character
  by whether the header or subject line was deliberately designed to leave the recipient without
  that information, or with misleading information, before they view the content, so a technically
  accurate but deliberately unrevealing design can still fail (§ 6 Abs. 2 DDG).
- A video-sharing platform's terms cannot treat the Abs. 3 uploader declaration function as
  discharging the platform's own Abs. 4 marking duty; the marking duty is triggered by the
  provider's own knowledge, from the declaration or any other source, and is not satisfied by an
  uploader's silence or a missing declaration (§ 6 Abs. 3, 4 DDG).
- A video-sharing platform's terms cannot rely on a general content policy or community guideline
  against unlawful advertising as satisfying § 11 Abs. 1; the duty is to secure an effective
  agreement with users on this specific prohibition, covering tobacco-product advertising under
  § 20 Tabakerzeugnisgesetz and medicinal-product advertising under § 10 Heilmittelwerbegesetz, not
  a broader or differently worded ban (§ 11 Abs. 1, 2 DDG).
- A document cannot assert that § 5 or § 6 exhausts the provider's information or communication
  duties; both sections expressly preserve other regimes (further statutory information duties
  under § 5 Abs. 2; the UWG and the Preisangabenverordnung under § 6 Abs. 5), so a statement that
  this file's items are the complete requirement understates the position (§ 5 Abs. 2, § 6 Abs. 5
  DDG).

## Product behaviour it assumes

- A single Impressum page exists and lists every § 5 Abs. 1 item that applies to the provider's
  actual legal form, licensing status, registration, and business, computed from the provider's own
  configuration (legal form, authorization status, register entry, profession, VAT or Economic ID,
  winding-up status, audiovisual-media status) rather than copied from a generic template
  (§ 5 Abs. 1 Nr. 1 to 8 DDG).
- The Impressum page stays leicht erkennbar and unmittelbar erreichbar as the service grows
  additional pages or flows, rather than becoming harder to find once the entry page it was built
  for is no longer the only one users land on (§ 5 Abs. 1 DDG chapeau). The source sets no fixed
  placement or click-count rule for this; a drafter should decide the concrete placement, see Open
  points to raise.
- The service stores, for the Nr. 2 duty, a working electronic-mail address and any other fast-
  contact channel it advertises, and keeps those current as contact details change (§ 5 Abs. 1 Nr. 2
  DDG).
- Where the provider's configuration changes (a new VAT ID, a change in winding-up status, a new
  register entry, a newly required authorization), the Impressum content updates with it rather than
  lagging behind a one-time setup (§ 5 Abs. 1 Nr. 4, 6, 7 DDG).
- Advertising, promotional offers, and competitions carry their own commercial-communication and
  promotion-condition labeling at the point where the user encounters them, rather than relying on a
  single disclosure placed only in general terms (§ 6 Abs. 1 Nr. 1 to 4 DDG).
- Outbound commercial email carries a header and subject line that name an identifiable
  sender and do not obscure the message's commercial character, as a check independent of the
  email's body content (§ 6 Abs. 2 DDG).
- A video-upload flow offers the uploader a control to declare whether the video contains
  audiovisual commercial communication, and the platform applies a commercial-communication marking
  to a video once it has learned of that content through the declaration or through any other
  channel (report, review, audit), rather than only through the declaration (§ 6 Abs. 3, 4 DDG).
- The terms a video-sharing platform's users accept include a clause binding them not to post
  audiovisual commercial communication that advertises tobacco products or advertises medicinal
  products in violation of § 10 Heilmittelwerbegesetz, and the acceptance flow records that
  agreement for every user rather than relying on an unenforced policy page (§ 11 Abs. 1, 2 DDG).

## Prescribed wording

None. § 5, § 6 and § 11 fix which categories of information, which labeling standards, and which
contractual outcome apply; none of them prescribes an exact sentence, label, or form of words to
reproduce.

## Open points to raise

- Whether the provider's actual legal form, licensing status, register entry, profession,
  VAT/Economic ID holding, winding-up status, and audiovisual-media status have been checked against
  the § 5 Abs. 1 list item by item, since an Impressum built from a generic template risks omitting
  an item that applies to this specific provider or including one that does not.
- Whether the Impressum page's placement meets unmittelbar erreichbar in practice across every
  entry point of the service, rather than only the page most commonly tested, given that the source
  states the standard without a numeric click-count or layout rule.
- Whether any outbound commercial email's header or subject line, considered on its own before the
  recipient opens the message, could be read as deliberately designed to withhold or mislead about
  the sender or the commercial character, independent of how clearly the body later discloses both.
- Whether a video-sharing platform's moderation or review process actually feeds discovered
  commercial content back into the Abs. 4 marking duty, or only into content-removal decisions,
  since the marking duty is triggered by the platform's knowledge regardless of its source.
- Whether the platform's user acceptance flow for the § 11 Abs. 1 prohibition amounts to an
  effective agreement (a clause every user affirmatively accepts) rather than a passive policy
  page, since the source requires the platform to secure the ban, not merely publish it.
