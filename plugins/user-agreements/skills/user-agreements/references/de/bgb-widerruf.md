# BGB right of withdrawal

Citation: BGB §§ 355 to 361, as last amended by Art. 6 G v. 23.7.2026 I Nr. 226
Version: Neugefasst durch Bek. v. 2.1.2002 I 42, 2909; 2003, 738
Official text: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/
Law as of: 2026-10-20

## When it applies

- Wherever a statute grants a consumer a Widerrufsrecht (right of withdrawal, see references/de/bgb-fernabsatz.md for when § 312g triggers it) under § 355, the consumer can release both sides from their contract-formation declarations by a timely, unambiguous declaration to the trader, needing no reasons; timely dispatch suffices to observe the period (§ 355 Abs. 1 BGB).
- The default period is 14 days from conclusion (§ 355 Abs. 2 BGB), but the following sections displace that start point by contract type; draft to the specific rule, never the blanket default, once the type is known. Goods purchase (Verbrauchsgüterkauf): receipt of the goods, or of the last good or shipment for a split delivery, or of the first good for regular delivery over a defined period (§ 356 Abs. 2 Nr. 1 Buchst. a bis d BGB). Water, gas, or electricity supplied neither in a limited volume nor in a fixed quantity, district heating, or non-physical digital content: conclusion (§ 356 Abs. 2 Nr. 2 BGB). Consumer loans: not before the borrower has the contract document or application; a document missing mandatory information starts the period only once that information is supplied, then running one month (§ 356b Abs. 1, 2 BGB). Timeshare and related products: conclusion of the (pre)contract, or later document receipt; withdrawal itself needs text form (§ 356c Abs. 1, 2 BGB). Instalment delivery outside distance/off-premises selling: not before EGBGB Art. 246 Abs. 3 information (§ 356d Abs. 1 BGB). Consumer construction: not before EGBGB Art. 249 § 3 instruction (§ 356e BGB). Gratuitous loan or financing assistance: not before § 514 Abs. 2 Satz 3 information (§ 356f BGB).
- In every case, the period does not begin at all before the relevant EGBGB information duty has actually been satisfied (§ 356 Abs. 3 BGB and the parallel rules in §§ 356b to 356f); a trader cannot assert the period has run while that information remains unsupplied.
- The right lapses at the latest 12 months and 14 days after the relevant start point for most contract types, including instalment delivery, consumer construction, and gratuitous loan or financing-assistance contracts, each measured from its own start point (§ 356 Abs. 4 Satz 1, § 356d Abs. 2, § 356e, § 356f BGB). A real-estate-secured loan lapses 12 months and 14 days after conclusion or the document disclosure, whichever is later (§ 356b Abs. 2 Satz 4 BGB); timeshare lapses at 3 months and 14 days where pre-contractual information was missing, extending to 12 months and 14 days where the withdrawal instruction itself was missing (§ 356c Abs. 3, 4 BGB). For a financial-service contract where the consumer was never instructed on the withdrawal right, no long-stop cutoff applies at all (§ 356 Abs. 4 Satz 2 BGB).

## What the documents must say

- Any distance contract (Fernabsatzvertrag) concluded via an Online-Benutzeroberfläche (online interface, meaning software including a website, part of one, or an application such as a mobile app): provide a Widerrufsfunktion (withdrawal function), permanently available, prominently placed, and easily accessible throughout the withdrawal period, letting the consumer provide or confirm without further ado their name, information identifying the contract or the part withdrawn from, and a return-communication channel (§ 356a Abs. 1, 2 BGB; labels under Prescribed wording). This is additive to the general right to withdraw by letter, email, or any other unambiguous means; it does not replace them (§ 355 Abs. 1 BGB).
- The function must be reachable from every subpage of the interface, directly or by a hyperlink that lands the consumer straight on the page to make the declaration; the duty applies equally whether the contract is concluded on the trader's own site or on a third-party platform, and the trader must bind the third-party operator to it by contract where needed (§ 356a Abs. 1 BGB). Where the function sits in a footer alongside terms, legal notice, or similar links, it needs its own distinguishing treatment, such as a different color, contrast, or a more prominent position, so a consumer does not read it as one more routine footer link (§ 356a Abs. 1 Satz 3 BGB, the color and contrast detail from the Begründung's elaboration of that Satz).
- The consumer must not be made to register or authenticate first merely to find or reach the function, and downloading an app is not required unless the contract itself was concluded through that app; the same goes for placing the function only inside a logged-in account area, unless the contract itself can only be concluded through that account. A non-registered guest order needs to be withdrawable as easily as it was placed, without the consumer first creating an account or logging in (§ 356a Abs. 1 BGB, drawn from the Begründung's discussion of that duty).
- Withdrawal proceeds in two steps: the consumer activates the withdrawal function itself, then supplies or confirms the three Abs. 2 data points and activates the separate confirmation function that actually transmits the declaration; no further review screen after that second activation is required (§ 356a Abs. 2, 3 BGB).
- Once that information is supplied, let the consumer transmit the declaration via a Bestätigungsfunktion (confirmation function) that actually submits it (§ 356a Abs. 3 BGB; label under Prescribed wording). On activation, send an immediate acknowledgment of receipt on a durable medium, with the declaration's content and the date and time of receipt (§ 356a Abs. 4 BGB); a declaration sent via the function before the period's expiry is deemed to have reached the trader in time (§ 356a Abs. 5 BGB).
- Word the acknowledgment so it states only that the declaration and its content, date, and time of receipt were received, without implying that the withdrawal's legal validity or scope has already been checked; a note that this review is still pending avoids that impression (§ 356a Abs. 4 BGB).
- The trader may let the consumer fill in and submit, on the trader's website, the EGBGB Anlage 2 model withdrawal form or another unambiguous withdrawal declaration; if the consumer uses this route, confirm receipt promptly on a durable medium (§ 356 Abs. 1 BGB).
- On withdrawal, restitute performance received without delay; a statutory maximum restitution period runs for the trader from receipt of the withdrawal and for the consumer from its dispatch, and the consumer observes it by timely dispatch of the goods; the trader bears the risk of the goods' return shipment (§ 355 Abs. 3 BGB).
- Off-premises and distance contracts excluding financial services: return performance received no later than 14 days after withdrawal, using the same payment means the consumer used unless expressly agreed otherwise at no cost to the consumer, and refund delivery payments except the extra cost of a pricier-than-standard delivery choice (§ 357 Abs. 1 to 3 BGB). Financial-service contracts: return performance no later than 30 days after withdrawal (§ 357b Abs. 1 BGB).
- For a Verbrauchsgüterkauf, the trader may withhold the refund until it has the goods back, or the consumer has proven dispatch, unless the trader offered to collect them itself; the trader must collect at its own cost where the goods cannot be sent back by post and were brought to the consumer's home at conclusion of an off-premises contract (§ 357 Abs. 4, 6, 7 BGB).
- For an instalment-delivery contract concluded neither at a distance nor off-premises, the general restitution rules in § 357 Abs. 1 to 4 and 6 apply by reference, but the consumer bears the direct cost of returning the goods unless the trader agreed to bear it, with the value-replacement rule of § 357a Abs. 1 applying on the basis of the Art. 246 Abs. 3 EGBGB instruction rather than the Art. 246a instruction (§ 357d BGB). For a Verbraucherbauvertrag, where returning the service received is impossible by its nature, the consumer owes value replacement instead, calculated on the agreed remuneration or, where that is disproportionately high, on the market value of the service rendered (§ 357e BGB).

## What the documents must not say

- A clause cannot demand a reason for withdrawal, impose a form stricter than § 355 itself (plus any specific channel such as § 356a), or start the period later than conclusion to the consumer's disadvantage absent a specific statutory displacement; nor shift the trader's return-shipment risk onto the consumer (§ 355 Abs. 1, 3, § 361 Abs. 2 BGB).
- A terms document cannot assert the period starts at conclusion for a Verbrauchsgüterkauf where § 356 Abs. 2 Nr. 1 Buchst. a bis d fixes it at receipt of the goods, nor assert the right has lapsed without tracking the exact combination of conditions the applicable section requires (§ 356 Abs. 2, 5, 6 BGB).
- A trader cannot substitute a different label for the two § 356a controls unless the substitute is equally unambiguous about its function, and cannot require the consumer to navigate away, log into an unrelated system, or supply unrelated data before the three § 356a Abs. 2 data points can be entered "ohne Weiteres" (without further ado) (§ 356a Abs. 2, 3 BGB).
- A footer placement that visually blends the withdrawal function into the surrounding terms, legal-notice, and similar links, with no distinguishing color, contrast, or position, does not satisfy the prominent-placement requirement (§ 356a Abs. 1 Satz 3 BGB, the color and contrast detail from the Begründung's elaboration of that Satz).
- An acknowledgment of receipt cannot be worded to suggest the withdrawal's legal validity or scope has already been reviewed, for example by stating the withdrawal itself is confirmed rather than only its receipt (§ 356a Abs. 4 BGB).
- A clause cannot shorten the 14-day (goods and services) or 30-day (financial services) restitution deadlines to the consumer's detriment, shift return-shipping cost to the consumer without having given the specific EGBGB information that conditions that shift, or charge value replacement for ordinary try-it-out handling of goods (§ 357 Abs. 1, 5, § 357a Abs. 1, § 357b Abs. 1 BGB).
- A clause claiming value replacement for withdrawal from non-physical digital content contradicts the flat, exception-free no-value-replacement rule in § 357a Abs. 3 BGB.
- A timeshare-adjacent document asserting the consumer owes any cost on withdrawal, beyond the narrow accommodation-misuse value replacement, contradicts the no-cost rule in § 357c Abs. 1, 2 BGB.
- A clause asserting that withdrawing a purchase leaves a linked financing loan in place, or vice versa, cannot override the fact-driven economic-unit test in § 358 Abs. 3 BGB; nor can a clause charge the consumer interest or unwinding costs on a linked loan after a § 358 Abs. 1 withdrawal (§ 358 Abs. 4 Satz 4 BGB).
- A linked-loan clause cannot override the consumer's separate right to withhold loan repayment where a defense against the linked contract's trader would justify withholding performance against that trader, once a demand for cure has failed where cure was available; this right does not apply to a loan financing the acquisition of financial instruments, or where the financed amount is under 200 euros (§ 359 Abs. 1, 2 BGB).
- No clause can assert a claim against the consumer beyond what this Untertitel states, including a differently labelled claim restating the same loss (§ 361 Abs. 1 BGB); and nothing in this file can be waived, shortened, or routed around to the consumer's detriment, directly or by alternative design (§ 361 Abs. 2 BGB).

## Product behaviour it assumes

- The withdrawal function and the confirmation control sit in the interface itself, not a one-off email address in a terms document, collecting the three data points directly in the flow without the consumer first needing to authenticate elsewhere or supply unrelated data (§ 356a Abs. 1 to 3 BGB).
- The flow runs the two steps in order: activating the withdrawal function, then supplying or confirming the three identification data points and activating the confirmation function, with no extra review screen inserted afterward (§ 356a Abs. 2, 3 BGB).
- A consumer who already identified themselves by logging in is not asked to identify themselves or the contract again; where the consumer holds several contracts, or one contract covers several goods or services, the flow lets them pick the specific contract or part from an order overview, so a partial withdrawal of only some items is possible (§ 356a Abs. 2 BGB).
- The function stays visible for the whole statutory withdrawal period on a blanket basis, even where the system cannot determine, for a specific consumer without disproportionate effort, exactly when that consumer's period runs out (for instance absent a login, or for data-protection reasons); showing the function at all times is not read as asserting a live right exists for that viewer, since the period itself is communicated separately after conclusion (§ 356a Abs. 1 BGB).
- Where the trader runs sales through its own site and a third-party platform, both channels carry the function, with the third-party operator bound to provide it by agreement (§ 356a Abs. 1 BGB).
- The system computes the withdrawal-period start by contract type (goods: receipt; utilities and non-physical digital content: conclusion; loans: document receipt; timeshare: (pre)contract conclusion or later document receipt), not by a single blanket rule, and withholds any statement that the period has started until the gating EGBGB information was actually given (§ 356 Abs. 2, 3 BGB).
- Where the trader wants early full performance to extinguish the withdrawal right, the flow collects, before performance starts, all the conditions together: for services, three (express consent to the early start, that consent on a durable medium for off-premises contracts, and a separate confirmation the consumer understands they lose the right, § 356 Abs. 5 Nr. 2 BGB); for non-physical digital content, those same three plus a § 312f confirmation given to the consumer (§ 356 Abs. 6 Nr. 2 BGB).
- Refund processing uses the consumer's original payment method by default, flags the standard-delivery-cost baseline so only the excess for a pricier delivery choice is withheld, and never applies value replacement to non-physical digital content under any circumstance (§ 357 Abs. 2, 3, § 357a Abs. 3 BGB).
- Checkout and cancellation logic flags a linked loan or connected service contract for automatic unwinding once the main contract is withdrawn, rather than requiring the consumer to separately contact a second trader or lender (§ 358 Abs. 4, § 360 Abs. 1 Satz 2 BGB).
- Records exist to prove, after the fact, when the withdrawal period actually started for a given consumer, since a dispute over the start date is resolved against the trader if it cannot prove the date (§ 361 Abs. 3 BGB).

## Prescribed wording

- PW-DE-4: see `references/prescribed-wording.md`. Quote: "Vertrag widerrufen" (§ 356a Abs. 1 Satz 2 BGB). Where it goes: the withdrawal function on the online interface of any distance contract, legibly labelled with this phrase or another equally unambiguous formulation; unlike PW-DE-1 to PW-DE-3, the statute does not require that nothing else appear on the control besides this phrase, only the label-or-equivalent standard.
- PW-DE-5: see `references/prescribed-wording.md`. Quote: "Widerruf bestätigen" (§ 356a Abs. 3 Satz 2 BGB). Where it goes: the confirmation function that actually transmits the withdrawal declaration, legibly labelled with this phrase or another equally unambiguous formulation; same lighter standard as PW-DE-4, no exclusivity requirement on what else the control may show.

## Open points to raise

- Whether a mandatory-login gate in front of the § 356a withdrawal function, where the three data points could otherwise be entered directly, raises the same concern as the parallel question for the § 312k Kündigungsbutton: pending before the Bundesgerichtshof, cases I ZR 272/25 and I ZR 275/25, hearing scheduled 5 November 2026 (see references/de/bgb-fernabsatz.md).
- Whether the product's contract type correctly maps to one of the specific period-start rules in § 356, § 356b to § 356f, since drafting to the 14-days-from-conclusion default where a specific rule applies understates or overstates the open window.
- Whether any purchase the business sells is financed by a linked loan, or paired with a connected service contract from the business or a cooperating third party, since the economic-unit test is fact-specific and a system hard-coded only for in-house financing will miss third-party-financed deals that still qualify through cooperation (§ 358 Abs. 3 BGB).
- Whether the business can produce, after the fact, proof of exactly when the EGBGB information that gates the withdrawal-period start was given to a specific consumer, given that a disputed start date is resolved against the trader (§ 361 Abs. 3 BGB).
- Whether any digital-content or service contract the business sells is meant to extinguish the withdrawal right on full performance, since all the conditions in § 356 Abs. 5 Nr. 2 (three) or Abs. 6 Nr. 2 (four, including the § 312f confirmation) must be collected together, and missing one leaves the right intact despite full delivery.
