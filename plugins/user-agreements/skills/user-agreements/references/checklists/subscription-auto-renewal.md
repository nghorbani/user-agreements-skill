# Subscription and auto-renewal checklist

Law as of: 2026-10-20

For a subscription with automatic renewal, monthly or annual, with or without a free trial or
intro price. Work the stages in order; where jurisdictions share a duty with different details,
each gets its own line. EU cites the Consumer Rights Directive, DE the BGB, CA Cal. Bus. and Prof.
Code, NY N.Y. Gen. Bus. Law, US federal ROSCA and the Negative Option Rule.

## Scope: before working the rest of this checklist

- [ ] Contract is not a distance financial-services contract; if it is, use the Art. 16a(1) information list and the Art. 16b withdrawal regime instead of Art. 6(1) and Art. 9 to 11a below (EU: Art. 3(1b); Art. 16a to 16e)
- [ ] Business is not one of the six exempt categories that remove the whole California regime (franchise or CPUC-licensed service; CPUC, FCC or FERC regulated affiliate; Department of Insurance entity; alarm company operator; bank, bank holding company or credit union; service contract seller or administrator) (CA: § 17605(a) to (f))
- [ ] Business is not one of the five exempt categories that remove the whole New York regime (franchise of a political subdivision; entity regulated by the Department of Financial Services; alarm system operator; bank, bank holding company or credit union; service contract seller or administrator) (NY: § 527-a(4)(a) to (e))
- [ ] The contract is not one the law requires a stricter cancellation form for, and is not a financial-services website or a financial-services contract, both of which take it outside the cancellation-button duty entirely (DE: § 312k Abs. 1 Satz 2 Nr. 1, 2 BGB)

## Offer and checkout page

- [ ] Total price per billing period (monthly cost if fixed-rate), duration, termination conditions and minimum obligation period shown before the consumer is bound (EU: Art. 6(1)(e), (o), (p))
- [ ] Renewal offer terms clear and conspicuous next to the consent request (CA: § 17602(a)(1))
- [ ] Offer's material terms clear and conspicuous before consent is requested (NY: § 527-a(1)(a))
- [ ] For a free trial, gift or intro price: how and when the price changes, stated clearly (EU: Art. 6(1)(e); CA: § 17602(a)(1); NY: § 527-a(1)(a))
- [ ] No contract term undermines the consumer's ability to consent (CA: § 17602(a)(5))
- [ ] A payment-method surcharge is void unless a free option exists and the fee does not exceed cost (DE: § 312a Abs. 4 BGB)
- [ ] No extra paid option pre-ticked as a default (DE: § 312a Abs. 3 Satz 2 BGB)
- [ ] The renewal term's initial duration does not exceed two years, and a silent renewal binds the consumer only onto an indefinite term with a right to end it on at most one month's notice (DE: § 309 Nr. 9 BGB)
- [ ] Before billing information is requested: a clear and conspicuous text disclosure of all material terms of the negative option feature (an internet-sold plan that treats silence as consent to keep paying) (US federal: 15 U.S.C. § 8403(1))

## Consent and the order button

- [ ] Order button for an electronic payment obligation carries the prescribed label or an unambiguous equivalent; see references/prescribed-wording.md, the PW-EU entries (EU: Art. 8(2))
- [ ] Order button for a German contract carries the prescribed label or an equally unambiguous formulation; see references/prescribed-wording.md, the PW-DE entries (DE: § 312j Abs. 3 BGB)
- [ ] Characteristics, price, durability label, duration/termination conditions and minimum obligation period shown again directly before the button; showing them earlier on the page is not enough (EU: Art. 8(2); points (a), (e), (la), (o), (p) of Art. 6(1))
- [ ] An input-error correction step given before submission (DE: § 312i Abs. 1 Nr. 1 BGB)
- [ ] Express affirmative consent captured before charging the initial, including promotional, term (CA: § 17602(a)(2); NY: § 527-a(1)(b))
- [ ] Express informed consent obtained before charging the consumer's card, debit or bank account for the negative option feature, by a distinct affirmative act separate from the rest of the transaction, with any consent checkbox rendered unchecked by default (US federal: 15 U.S.C. § 8403(2); FTC 2021 negative option guidance)
- [ ] A simple mechanism for the consumer to stop recurring charges exists and is wired to actually work once invoked (US federal: 15 U.S.C. § 8403(3))

## Confirmation after the order

- [ ] Contract confirmed on a durable medium within a reasonable time, at the latest by delivery or before service starts (EU: Art. 8(7))
- [ ] Electronic order-receipt confirmation sent promptly (DE: § 312i Abs. 1 Nr. 3 BGB)
- [ ] Retainable acknowledgment of offer terms and how to cancel; a trial also lets the consumer cancel before paying (CA: § 17602(a)(3))
- [ ] Retainable notice sent promptly after consent, covering terms, cost, frequency and cancellation (NY: § 527-a(1)(c))

## During the subscription: reminders and trial conversion

- [ ] Free trial, gift or promo price over 31 days: pre-billing notice 3 to 21 days before it ends (CA: § 17602(a)(8), (b)(1))
- [ ] Free gift or trial over 1 month, then a renewal charge: reminder 3 to 21 days before that cancellation deadline (NY: § 527-a(1)(h))
- [ ] Initial term of 1 year or more that auto-renews: notice 15 to 45 days before renewal (CA: § 17602(a)(8), (b)(2))
- [ ] Initial paid term of 1 year or more renewing 6 months or more: reminder 15 to 45 days before the cancellation deadline (NY: § 527-a(1)(f))
- [ ] Annual plan: yearly reminder naming the product, charge and cancellation means (CA: § 17602(h))
- [ ] Shipment without prior affirmative consent is an unconditional gift: no charge, no return cost (CA: § 17603; NY: § 527-a(2))

## Price increases and other material changes

- [ ] Clear, conspicuous, retainable notice of a material change plus how to cancel, before it takes effect (CA: § 17602(g)(1))
- [ ] A fee change specifically is notified 7 to 30 days before it takes effect (CA: § 17602(g)(2))
- [ ] A material change, including a price increase, is notified 5 business days to 30 days before it takes effect (NY: § 527-a(1)(g))
- [ ] Before a higher charge than disclosed: consent to the increase, or a 14-day post-charge cancel window with pro rata refund (NY: § 527-a(1)(b-1))
- [ ] Information given under Art. 6(1) is not altered after conclusion without express agreement (EU: Art. 6(5))
- [ ] Any clause letting the trader change a continuing obligation's terms, including price, through the consumer's silence rather than an actual accepted offer is avoided; a matching termination right does not cure an open-ended deemed-consent design (DE: § 307 Abs. 1 Satz 1, Abs. 2 Nr. 1 BGB; BGH, judgment of 27 April 2021, XI ZR 26/20)

## Cancellation: the path, the button, the confirmation

- [ ] All five German prescribed labels checked together as one set before launch: zahlungspflichtig bestellen, Verträge hier kündigen, jetzt kündigen, Vertrag widerrufen, Widerruf bestätigen; see references/prescribed-wording.md, the PW-DE entries (DE: § 312j, § 312k, § 356a BGB)
- [ ] A permanently available cancellation button carries the prescribed label and sits on the site, leading directly to a confirmation page that asks for cancellation type, identification, contract designation, end date and a confirmation channel; see references/prescribed-wording.md, the PW-DE entries (DE: § 312k Abs. 1, 2 BGB)
- [ ] No login step between the cancellation button and the confirmation page and no password field on it: the Kammergericht held both unlawful, and the BGH hears the appeals on 5 November 2026 (DE: § 312k Abs. 2 Satz 3, 4 BGB; KG 5 UKl 10/25 and 5 U 6/25; BGH I ZR 272/25, I ZR 275/25)
- [ ] That page ends with a second, prescribed-label button; the consumer can store the dated declaration on a durable medium; see references/prescribed-wording.md, the PW-DE entries (DE: § 312k Abs. 2 Satz 3 Nr. 2, Abs. 3 BGB)
- [ ] The confirmation page shows nothing beyond the statutory fields and that button: no retention offer, no pause option, no other information (DE: § 312k Abs. 2 Satz 3 BGB; BGH, judgment of 16 July 2026, I ZR 200/25)
- [ ] The confirmation page does not force an end-date entry: the field lets the consumer state one, it does not require one (DE: § 312k Abs. 2 Satz 3 Nr. 1 Buchst. d, Abs. 5 BGB)
- [ ] A missing or non-compliant button lets the consumer cancel at any time, no notice period, while the defect lasts (DE: § 312k Abs. 6 BGB)
- [ ] Online-only cancellation works at will, no obstructing step, if online acceptance is offered; login required only for an existing account (CA: § 17602(d))
- [ ] A phone, email, postal or other easy channel exists; calls answered promptly; voicemail resolved within 1 business day (CA: § 17602(c))
- [ ] A retention offer appears only with the required trigger: by phone, after disclosing the fixed trigger word that cancels at once; online, alongside the fixed-label cancel control (CA: § 17602(e))
- [ ] Cancellation available in the activation medium or the consumer's accustomed medium; a phone number stays available if activation was by phone (CA: § 17602(f))
- [ ] Cancellation offered at any time, as easily as and through every medium used for consent (NY: § 527-a(1)(d), (d-1))
- [ ] No unreasonable condition on cancellation; never refused, obstructed or delayed; a retention offer never blocks it (NY: § 527-a(1)(e))
- [ ] Nothing in the flow waives or narrows these duties, directly or by workaround (DE: § 312m Abs. 1 BGB)
- [ ] A retention offer or discount pitch may follow a cancellation request but never precede or gate it; a single offer is not itself unreasonable delay, but a repeated pitch or ignoring a decline is (US federal: 15 U.S.C. § 8403(3); FTC 2021 negative option guidance)
- [ ] The cancellation mechanism is at least as easy to use as the sign-up method and reachable through at least the same medium used to sign up (US federal: 15 U.S.C. § 8403(3); FTC 2021 negative option guidance)

## The EU right of withdrawal

- [ ] 14 days to withdraw, no reason needed, from conclusion for a service or digital content subscription, unless an Art. 16 exception applies (EU: Art. 9(1), (2))
- [ ] If the trader never gave the Art. 6(1)(h) notice, the period extends to 12 months from the original period's end (EU: Art. 10(1))
- [ ] Withdrawal right, procedure and time limit disclosed before conclusion, via the Annex I(B) form or correctly filled Annex I(A) instructions (EU: Art. 6(1)(h); Art. 6(4))
- [ ] For an online-interface subscription: a persistent, prescribed-label withdrawal function throughout the period, plus a second, prescribed-label confirmation control with a durable-medium acknowledgment; see references/prescribed-wording.md, the PW-EU entries (EU: Art. 11a(1), (3), (4))
- [ ] Any early-performance request during the withdrawal period, and the related acknowledgment, were obtained and recorded (EU: Art. 8(8))
- [ ] For digital content excluding withdrawal once performance starts: prior express consent, acknowledgment of losing the right, and confirmation were obtained (EU: Art. 16(m))
- [ ] For a German distance contract concluded via an online interface: the withdrawal function reachable from every subpage, without a registration or login gate merely to reach it, letting the consumer supply or confirm name, contract-identifying details and a return-communication channel without further ado; see references/prescribed-wording.md, the PW-DE entries (DE: § 356a Abs. 1, 2 BGB)
- [ ] The withdrawal function, if placed in a footer with other links, carries its own distinguishing color, contrast or position so it is not read as a routine footer link (DE: § 356a Abs. 1 Satz 3 BGB)
- [ ] A second, separate confirmation function actually transmits the declaration; on activation, an immediate durable-medium acknowledgment states only that the declaration and its content, date and time were received, without implying the withdrawal's validity was already reviewed; see references/prescribed-wording.md, the PW-DE entries (DE: § 356a Abs. 3, 4 BGB)
- [ ] Where the trader seeks to rely on full early performance to extinguish the withdrawal right: all the conditions for that contract type were collected together before performance started (for a service, three; for non-physical digital content, those three plus the § 312f confirmation) (DE: § 356 Abs. 5 Nr. 2, Abs. 6 Nr. 2 BGB)

## Records

- [ ] Record of what pre-contractual information was shown and when: the burden of proof sits with the trader (EU: Art. 6(9))
- [ ] Record of consent to the renewal offer kept 3 years, or 1 year past termination, whichever is longer (CA: § 17602(a)(4), (a)(6))
- [ ] Record of which information duties were met: the trader bears the burden of proof, not the consumer the burden of disproof (DE: § 312m Abs. 2 BGB)
- [ ] Record of when the withdrawal period actually started for a given consumer: a disputed start date is resolved against the trader if it cannot prove the date (DE: § 361 Abs. 3 BGB)
- [ ] Record of the medium used for sign-up to a negative option feature, so cancellation can be shown to run through at least that same medium (US federal: 15 U.S.C. § 8403(3); FTC 2021 negative option guidance)

## Not covered here

The national rules of EU member states other than Germany, US states other than California and
New York, and the Telemarketing Sales Rule's own disclosure duties for a telephone-sold negative
option feature. Those come as separate files later in this skill.
