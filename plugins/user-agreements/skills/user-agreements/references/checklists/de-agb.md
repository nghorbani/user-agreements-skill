# German AGB checklist

Law as of: 2026-10-20

For a trader using standard contract terms (Allgemeine Geschäftsbedingungen) toward a party in
Germany, consumer or business. Checks the German layer: inclusion and content control under
§§ 305 to 310 BGB, the e-commerce and cancellation-button duties of §§ 312 to 312m BGB, the
digital-products regime of §§ 327 to 327u BGB, the sale and consumer-goods-sale rules of
§§ 433 to 445c and 474 to 479g BGB, the dispute-resolution notice of the VSBG, and the Impressum
and commercial-communication duties of the DDG. The EU directive floor behind these rules is
checked separately (see the eu-b2c checklist).

## Before drafting: classify the relationship and the contract

- [ ] Counterparty classified as Verbraucher or Unternehmer by the purpose of the specific
      transaction, not the person's general occupation; a terms document does not narrow either
      definition to strip a natural person of consumer status for an out-of-trade transaction (DE:
      § 13, § 14, § 312m Abs. 1 BGB)
- [ ] Where the counterparty is a business, checked further for a named financial-sector business
      (banking, payment-services, investment, capital-management or stock-exchange business under
      the named sector statutes), a großer Unternehmer (headcount, revenue or balance-sheet
      threshold), a utility special customer, or a listed public-sector or supranational body,
      since each removes some or all of §§ 305 Abs. 2, 3, 308, 309 from the relationship (DE: § 310
      Abs. 1, 1a, 2 BGB)
- [ ] Against a consumer, the whole Abschnitt applies with three modifications: AGB count as put
      forward by the trader unless the consumer introduced them; a preformulated term meant for
      one-off use still gets § 305c Abs. 2 and §§ 306, 307 to 309 treatment where the consumer could
      not influence its content because it was preformulated; and assessing unreasonable
      disadvantage under § 307 Abs. 1, 2 also weighs in the circumstances surrounding conclusion (DE:
      § 310 Abs. 3 BGB)
- [ ] Contract checked against the excluded-area list (inheritance, family and company law,
      collective-bargaining and works agreements) and against employment, which keeps the
      Abschnitt but drops the § 305 Abs. 2, 3 inclusion conditions (DE: § 310 Abs. 4 BGB)
- [ ] Consumer contract checked against the § 312 Abs. 2 to 6 narrowed buckets (notarized deals,
      land-rights transfers, consumer construction, social services, residential tenancies,
      timeshare, medical treatment, everyday-goods rounds, vending machines, payphones, single
      telecom connections, low-value off-premises deals under 40 euros paid on the spot, forced
      sales, passenger transport, insurance, financial-service series, package travel) before
      applying the full Chapter 1/2 duty set (DE: § 312 Abs. 2 to 6 BGB)
- [ ] Off-premises contract (physical presence away from business premises, or immediately
      preceding such contact) and distance contract (exclusively distance-communication means,
      within an organized distance-sale system) correctly told apart, since the duties below attach
      differently (DE: § 312b Abs. 1, § 312c Abs. 1, 2 BGB)
- [ ] Product sorted as a digital product (digital content or digital service against a price or
      against personal data, under § 327), a Ware with digital elements (goods that need the
      digital component to function, under § 327a Abs. 3), or an ordinary Ware, since the three
      tracks carry different conformity, remedy and limitation rules (DE: § 327 Abs. 1, 3, 5, 6,
      § 327a Abs. 1 to 3 BGB)
- [ ] Where personal data is supplied as or alongside a price, checked whether the trader processes
      it only to perform its own contractual or legal duty; any further use, including advertising,
      brings the full consumer-contract and digital-product regimes into play regardless of a
      nominal price of zero (DE: § 312 Abs. 1a, § 327 Abs. 3 i.V.m. § 312 Abs. 1a Satz 2 BGB)

## Standard terms: inclusion and wording control

- [ ] A term counts as AGB once it is preformulated for a multitude of contracts and one party
      presents it to the other at conclusion, whatever document it sits in, its length or its
      typeface; a point-by-point negotiated term is not AGB at all (DE: § 305 Abs. 1 BGB)
- [ ] Inclusion shown as an actual pointer or notice, rather than terms merely published somewhere: an express pointer or, where
      disproportionately difficult, a clearly visible on-site notice; a reasonable chance to learn
      the content, accounting for a physical impairment the trader could recognize; and the other
      party's agreement to the terms applying (DE: § 305 Abs. 2 BGB)
- [ ] Against a business counterparty or a public-law body, the § 305 Abs. 2 inclusion conditions
      do not apply; inclusion is checked on ordinary contract-formation grounds instead (DE: § 310
      Abs. 1 BGB)
- [ ] No individually negotiated term is overridden by a conflicting AGB term, whatever the AGB
      says on the point; an unusual term the other party need not expect under the circumstances
      and the contract's outward appearance is flagged as a non-inclusion risk rather than a mere
      invalidity risk (DE: § 305b, § 305c Abs. 1 BGB)
- [ ] Ambiguous wording is resolved in the drafter's own review against the drafter, since a
      remaining interpretive doubt is read against the Verwender in any dispute; an unclear or
      hard-to-follow term is checked even where its substance would otherwise survive content
      control (DE: § 305c Abs. 2, § 307 Abs. 3 BGB)
- [ ] A term that departs from or adds to a statutory rule, even where it matches no specific § 308
      or § 309 category, is checked against the general unreasonable-disadvantage standard, with the
      circumstances at conclusion weighed in against a consumer (DE: § 307 Abs. 1, 2, § 310 Abs. 1, 3
      BGB)
- [ ] A severability clause, alternative structure, relabelling or restructuring is not relied on to
      do § 306's job: where a term fails, the statute already keeps the contract alive and fills the
      gap with statutory law unless that is an unreasonable hardship, and no alternative arrangement
      achieves by another route what this Abschnitt bars directly (DE: §§ 306, 306a BGB)

## Termination, release and change-of-performance clauses

- [ ] A release right conditioned on the other party's own stated, justified reason is drafted with
      that reason actually named in the contract, not left to be supplied later (continuing
      obligations carved out) (DE: § 308 Nr. 3 BGB)
- [ ] A reserved right to change or deviate from the promised performance states the limits of that
      right, so that accepting the change, weighing the Verwender's own interests, stays reasonable
      for the other party (DE: § 308 Nr. 4 BGB)
- [ ] A deemed-silence clause gives the other party a reasonable period to make an express
      declaration instead, and flags, at the start of that period, what the silence or inaction is
      specifically deemed to mean (DE: § 308 Nr. 5 Buchst. a, b BGB)
- [ ] No mechanism lets the business change ongoing contract terms, including a price or fee change
      for a continuing obligation, through the customer's mere silence with no substantive cap on
      what can change or by how much; a matching termination right offered alongside such a clause
      does not cure it, and this holds even where a separate statute such as § 675g Abs. 2 BGB also
      permits a deemed-consent mechanism for the same change (DE: § 305 Abs. 2, § 311 Abs. 1, § 307
      Abs. 1 Satz 1, Abs. 2 Nr. 1 BGB; BGH, judgment of 27 April 2021, XI ZR 26/20)
- [ ] A release right reserved for unavailability of performance commits to notify the other party
      of the unavailability without delay and to refund any counter-performance without delay (DE:
      § 308 Nr. 8 Buchst. a, b BGB)
- [ ] A reserved compensation-for-use or expense claim after withdrawal or termination by either
      side is set at a reasonable, cost-based level, not an unreasonably high flat sum (DE: § 308
      Nr. 7 Buchst. a, b BGB)
- [ ] An auto-renewal clause for a recurring-supply or recurring-service continuing obligation
      extends the contract only onto an indefinite term, gives a contractual right to end the
      renewed contract at any time on at most one month's notice, and leaves any statutory
      termination right standing alongside, not in its place (DE: § 309 Nr. 9 Buchst. b BGB)
- [ ] A subscription's initial term, renewal mechanism and termination notice period are checked
      together, not clause by clause: the initial term does not exceed two years, renewal binds the
      other party only once more unless it is onto an indefinite term with the matching right
      above, and the pre-renewal notice period does not exceed one month before the end of the
      initial term; contracts for goods customarily sold together, and insurance contracts, sit
      outside this whole category (DE: § 309 Nr. 9 BGB)
- [ ] No clause bars assignment of a right the other party holds against the Verwender (beyond a
      money claim) unless the Verwender has a legitimate interest outweighing the other party's
      interest in assignability (DE: § 308 Nr. 9 Buchst. b BGB)

## Payment, performance timing and liability clauses

- [ ] No clause sets an unreasonably long or insufficiently determined period for the Verwender to
      accept or reject an offer, or to perform (a reservation to perform only after the § 355
      withdrawal period has run is carved out) (DE: § 308 Nr. 1 BGB)
- [ ] Against a non-consumer, no clause reserves more than 30 days after receiving the
      counter-performance or the invoice, whichever is later, to satisfy a payment claim, and no
      clause reserves more than 15 days after receiving the counter-performance where payment
      depends on first inspecting or accepting it (DE: § 308 Nr. 1a, 1b BGB)
- [ ] No clause reserves an unreasonably long or insufficiently determined Nachfrist for the
      Verwender's own performance (DE: § 308 Nr. 2 BGB)
- [ ] No clause treats a payment-received or receipt declaration as deemed access for a declaration
      of particular importance to the Verwender (DE: § 308 Nr. 6 BGB)
- [ ] No clause charges a price increase for goods or services due within four months of conclusion
      (continuing obligations carved out) (DE: § 309 Nr. 1 BGB)
- [ ] No clause excludes or restricts the statutory right to refuse performance or the retention
      right from the same contractual relationship, including by conditioning retention on the
      Verwender's own acknowledgment of the defect, and no clause strips the power to set off an
      undisputed or finally established claim (DE: § 309 Nr. 2 Buchst. a, b, Nr. 3 BGB)
- [ ] No clause excuses the Verwender from the statutory reminder or Nachfrist duty before the
      other party's default remedies apply (DE: § 309 Nr. 4 BGB)
- [ ] A flat compensation or diminished-value clause does not exceed the ordinarily expected damage
      and expressly keeps the other party's right to prove a lower or no loss (DE: § 309 Nr. 5
      Buchst. a, b BGB)
- [ ] No clause imposes a contractual penalty for non-acceptance, late acceptance, payment default,
      or the other party's own withdrawal (DE: § 309 Nr. 6 BGB)
- [ ] No clause excludes or limits liability for death, personal injury or harm to health from a
      negligent breach by the Verwender or an intentional or negligent breach by its statutory
      representative or vicarious agent, with no carve-out of any kind for this category; no clause
      excludes or limits liability for other damage from a grossly negligent breach, subject only to
      the named transport-tariff and state-approved-lottery carve-outs (DE: § 309 Nr. 7 Buchst. a, b
      BGB)
- [ ] For newly manufactured goods or work services, no clause excludes or limits defect claims,
      redirects them to a third party, confines them to supplementary performance alone without
      keeping a price-reduction or withdrawal right, excludes or limits the duty to bear
      supplementary-performance expenses, conditions supplementary performance on advance payment
      disproportionate to the defect, shortens the notice cut-off for a non-obvious defect, or
      shortens the limitation period below the statutory floor (DE: § 309 Nr. 8 Buchst. a, b,
      Doppelbuchst. aa to ff BGB)
- [ ] No clause lets a third party step into the Verwender's contractual position without naming
      that party or giving the other party a right to leave (DE: § 309 Nr. 10 BGB)
- [ ] No clause imposes personal liability or a guarantee obligation on an agent without an express,
      separate declaration, and no clause extends an unauthorized agent's liability beyond § 179 BGB
      (DE: § 309 Nr. 11 Buchst. a, b BGB)
- [ ] No clause shifts the burden of proof to the other party's disadvantage, including by having
      the other party confirm particular facts (a separately signed or separately
      qualified-electronically-signed receipt acknowledgment is carved out) (DE: § 309 Nr. 12
      Buchst. a, b BGB)
- [ ] No clause binds a notice or declaration owed to the Verwender to a stricter form than
      Textform (or written form or notarization where the law itself requires it), and no clause
      confines a consumer to a single electronic channel in place of every other lawful way of
      declaring, including the § 312k cancellation channel (DE: § 309 Nr. 13 Buchst. a, b, c BGB)
- [ ] No clause requires an out-of-court settlement attempt before the other party may sue, and no
      works-contract clause demands instalment payments or reduces security below the §§ 632a,
      650m BGB floor (DE: § 309 Nr. 14, Nr. 15 Buchst. a, b BGB)

## Checkout and the order button

- [ ] Off-premises and distance contracts carry the EGBGB Art. 246a pre-contract information as
      contract content, or, for financial services, the Art. 246b §§ 1 to 3 information with the
      online interface designed per Art. 246b § 4; costs are demanded from the consumer only to the
      extent the trader informed the consumer of them beforehand (DE: § 312d, § 312e BGB)
- [ ] Delivery restrictions and accepted payment means stated no later than the start of the
      ordering process (DE: § 312j Abs. 1 BGB)
- [ ] The EGBGB Art. 246a Abs. 1 Satz 1 Nr. 1, 5 to 8, 11a, 14 and 15 information (price, main
      characteristics, delivery, duration, minimum term and the rest of that list) shown clearly,
      comprehensibly and in a highlighted manner immediately before order submission, not behind a
      separate terms link (DE: § 312j Abs. 2 BGB)
- [ ] Input-error recognition and correction offered before submission, the EGBGB Art. 246c
      information given in good time, and retrievable, storable contract terms kept at conclusion;
      the first three duties fall away only for a contract concluded exclusively through individual
      communication, never the fourth (DE: § 312i Abs. 1, 2 BGB)
- [ ] The order-submission button carries PW-DE-1 or an equally unambiguous formulation, read on its
      own without help from price information or a terms link shown earlier on the page, so that an
      average reasonably well informed and reasonably observant and circumspect consumer understands
      that activating it creates a payment obligation; a soft, reservation-flavored verb is flagged
      as a drafting risk for this reason (DE: § 312j Abs. 3 Satz 2 BGB; Case C-249/21,
      Fuhrmann-2-GmbH v B., judgment of 7 April 2022, paras. 26, 28 to 30, 33)
- [ ] No extra-payment agreement beyond the main price is reached through a pre-ticked default; any
      such agreement needs the trader's express agreement and otherwise never becomes part of the
      contract (DE: § 312a Abs. 3 BGB)
- [ ] A payment-method surcharge does not exceed the trader's actual cost of that method, and a
      customary, reasonable free payment option exists alongside any surcharged one; a premium-rate
      support-line fee does not exceed the pure telecommunications cost (DE: § 312a Abs. 4 to 6 BGB)
- [ ] Where the trader or someone calling in its name phones the consumer to conclude a contract,
      the caller discloses its own identity, the identity of whoever it calls for, and the call's
      commercial purpose at the start of the conversation (DE: § 312a Abs. 1 BGB)

## Confirmation after the order

- [ ] Off-premises conclusion: a signed contract copy or confirmation reproducing the contract
      content, including the EGBGB Art. 246a information not already given pre-contractually,
      handed over promptly on paper or, with consent, another durable medium (DE: § 312f Abs. 1
      BGB)
- [ ] Distance conclusion: the same confirmation on a durable medium within a reasonable time, at
      the latest on delivery or before the service starts (DE: § 312f Abs. 2 BGB)
- [ ] For digital content not on a physical medium, the copy or confirmation also records any
      express consent to an early start and the confirmed knowledge that this means losing the
      withdrawal right (DE: § 312f Abs. 3 BGB)
- [ ] Records kept of what information was shown to a given consumer and when, since the trader
      bears the burden of proving an information duty was met (DE: § 312m Abs. 2 BGB)
- [ ] An online marketplace operator gives the EGBGB Art. 246d information the operator role
      requires, on top of its own terms (DE: § 312l Abs. 1 BGB)

## Cancellation button and flow

- [ ] A website letting consumers conclude a continuing paid contract carries a cancellation button,
      permanently available, immediately and easily accessible, carrying PW-DE-2 with nothing else
      on the button, routing directly to a confirmation page; where a third party runs the website,
      the trader's contract with that operator obliges it to provide the mechanism (DE: § 312k
      Abs. 1, 2, Abs. 2 Satz 2, 4 BGB)
- [ ] The confirmation page asks only for: cancellation type and, for extraordinary cancellation,
      the reason; unambiguous identification; unambiguous contract designation; the desired end
      date, which the consumer may state but is never forced to; and an electronic channel for the
      confirmation; no further field, offer or information, such as a retention offer or a pause
      option, is added (DE: § 312k Abs. 2 Satz 3 Nr. 1 Buchst. a to e BGB; BGH, judgment of 16 July
      2026, I ZR 200/25, BGH press release 128/2026)
- [ ] The confirmation page carries a second, separate button with PW-DE-3 and nothing else,
      submitting the cancellation declaration (DE: § 312k Abs. 2 Satz 3 Nr. 2 BGB)
- [ ] Both buttons and the confirmation page are checked against a design that routes through a
      login step, such as a customer number and password; the point is pending before the BGH, so a
      flow without a login step is the safer design until it is settled (DE: § 312k Abs. 2 Satz 3,
      4 BGB; KG, decisions of 18 and 11 November 2025, 5 UKl 10/25 and 5 U 6/25; BGH hearing of
      5 November 2026, I ZR 272/25 and I ZR 275/25, BGH press release 133/2026)
- [ ] The flow and the confirmation page stay reachable at any time, including during a short
      maintenance outage, with no gap, however brief, since any failure exposes the affected
      contract to an any-time, no-notice cancellation (DE: § 312k Abs. 2 Satz 4, Abs. 6 BGB)
- [ ] The consumer can store their own timestamped cancellation declaration on a durable medium,
      showing it was made by activating the confirmation button; a declaration made that way is
      presumed to have reached the trader immediately (DE: § 312k Abs. 3, Abs. 4 Satz 2 BGB)
- [ ] Receipt is confirmed to the consumer immediately, electronically, in text form, stating
      content, timestamp and the resulting end date; where no end date was stated, cancellation
      takes effect at the earliest possible time (DE: § 312k Abs. 4 Satz 1, Abs. 5 BGB)
- [ ] A cancellation-by-proxy clause under § 312h does not require less than text form for the
      consumer's cancellation declaration or the power of attorney to cancel (DE: § 312h BGB)
- [ ] The duty is checked against the two statutory carve-outs (a contract whose cancellation the
      law itself requires in stricter form, such as written form or notarization; a
      financial-services website or contract) before being applied at all (DE: § 312k Abs. 1 Satz 2
      Nr. 1, 2 BGB)
- [ ] A distance contract's withdrawal right is checked separately from this cancellation-button set:
      § 312g BGB carries the right itself and its mechanics and effects, in references/de/bgb-widerruf.md
      (DE: § 312g Abs. 1 BGB)
- [ ] A distance contract concluded through an online interface (a website, part of one, or an app)
      carries a withdrawal function: permanently available, prominently placed, reachable from every
      subpage without forcing the consumer to register or log in first, letting the consumer supply or
      confirm their name, the contract or part withdrawn from, and a return-communication channel
      without further ado; the control carries PW-DE-4 or an equally unambiguous formulation (DE:
      § 356a Abs. 1, 2 BGB)
- [ ] The withdrawal function routes to a second, separate control that actually transmits the
      declaration, carrying PW-DE-5 or an equally unambiguous formulation, followed by an immediate
      acknowledgment of receipt on a durable medium, timestamped, worded so it confirms only the
      declaration's receipt and content and does not imply the withdrawal's legal validity has already
      been checked (DE: § 356a Abs. 3, 4 BGB)

## Digital products: supply, conformity and updates

- [ ] The moment of supply is defined as making the digital content, or the means to access or
      retrieve it, available or accessible to the consumer, or making the digital service
      accessible; no clause describes supply as happening only on the consumer's first login or
      first use instead (DE: § 327b Abs. 3, 4 BGB)
- [ ] The product is classified as a one-off supply, a series of individual supplies, or a
      continuous supply over the agreed period, since the classification drives the burden of
      proof, the limitation start date and the conformity test (DE: § 327b Abs. 5, § 327e Abs. 1
      Satz 3 BGB)
- [ ] Agreed characteristics (quantity, functionality, compatibility, interoperability), fitness for
      the assumed use, accessories, instructions and support, and any agreed updates are stated as
      binding product terms, not left as marketing language the product can fall short of (DE:
      § 327e Abs. 2 BGB)
- [ ] The version supplied and the update commitment are stated; absent other agreement, the
      product is in the newest version available at conclusion, and conformity updates, including
      security updates, are supplied with the consumer informed of them for the whole supply period
      or the period a reasonable consumer would expect for that kind of product (DE: § 327e Abs. 3
      Satz 1 Nr. 5, 6, § 327f Abs. 1 BGB)
- [ ] Any deviation from the objective requirements, the update duty, or the legal-defect standard
      is captured through its own express, separate active step (a dedicated checkbox or
      confirmation button) tied to the specific feature; silence, a term inside standard conditions,
      a pre-ticked box, or later ratification does not qualify (DE: § 327h BGB)
- [ ] No clause shifts the burden of proving supply onto the consumer, and no clause shifts the
      burden of disproving a defect existed at supply onto the consumer within the statutory window,
      except where the trader proves an incompatible digital environment against disclosed technical
      requirements, or a failed, reasonable, least-invasive cooperation request (DE: § 327b Abs. 6,
      § 327k Abs. 1 to 4 BGB)
- [ ] A disadvantageous change to a continuously-supplied product names the good reason relied on,
      states there is no added cost to the consumer, and gives clear advance information of the
      change's features, timing and the consumer's 30-day termination right, or the option to keep
      the current version at no extra cost, before the change takes effect (DE: § 327r Abs. 1 to 3
      BGB)
- [ ] No clause makes continuing the contract, or any payment obligation, conditional on the
      consumer not exercising a data-protection right or not withdrawing consent, and no clause
      claims a damages or reimbursement right against the consumer for the resulting narrower
      processing scope (DE: § 327q Abs. 1, 3 BGB)
- [ ] A post-termination content-retention clause claims only the four named categories (content
      with no outside use, content tied solely to the product, content aggregated and not
      separable, content jointly created for other users' continued benefit); anything else is
      handed back free of charge, unhindered, in reasonable time and in a common machine-readable
      format (DE: § 327p Abs. 2, 3 BGB)
- [ ] No refund clause names an alternative payment method that costs the consumer more than the
      original method would have, and no clause adds a use-compensation charge alongside the
      price-reduction or termination refund mechanics (DE: § 327n Abs. 4 BGB)
- [ ] No agreement reached before the consumer's notice of non-supply or a defect deviates from the
      digital-products subtitle to the consumer's disadvantage, and no alternative structure evades
      it either; an agreed exclusion or limit on damages claims and a valid § 327h deviation stay
      available on their own separate terms (DE: § 327s Abs. 1 to 5 BGB)
- [ ] A B2B distribution-chain record traces which upstream Vertriebspartner supplied a given
      digital product, so a trader facing a consumer claim can pursue its own recourse claim (DE:
      § 327t, § 327u BGB)

## Goods: conformity, defects and consumer-sales rules

- [ ] Agreed condition, presupposed use, and included accessories and instructions are stated
      explicitly, since whatever is fixed there becomes the subjective-requirement benchmark ahead
      of the objective fallback (DE: § 434 Abs. 2 BGB)
- [ ] A departure from an objective requirement (ordinary use, usual condition, sample match,
      expected accessories), for a consumer, is both specifically disclosed before the consumer's
      contractual declaration and recorded in an express, separate contract term; neither step
      works once notice of an actual defect has reached the business (DE: § 434 Abs. 3 Satz 1,
      § 476 Abs. 1 Satz 2 BGB)
- [ ] Before Nacherfüllung for a consumer, the consumer is told of the choice between repair and
      replacement and that cure by repair extends the limitation period once, by twelve months (DE:
      § 475 Abs. 4 BGB)
- [ ] Where no performance time is fixed for a consumer sale, handover is due no later than 30 days
      after conclusion (DE: § 475 Abs. 1 BGB)
- [ ] A consumer sale's limitation period for a defect claim is checked against the full layered set
      beyond the general two-year or one-year floor: twelve months after continuous digital-element
      provision ends, twelve months after an update-duty period ends, four months after a defect first
      shows up within the period, two months after a cured item is handed back, and a one-time
      twelve-month extension once Nacherfüllung is rendered by repair (DE: § 475e Abs. 1 to 5 BGB)
- [ ] The buyer's own actual or grossly-negligent-missed knowledge of a defect at conclusion excludes
      the buyer's remedies for it, unless the seller fraudulently concealed the defect or gave its own
      Garantie on the item's condition, in which case the remedies survive despite that negligence
      (DE: § 442 Abs. 1 BGB)
- [ ] For a Ware with digital elements, the contract fixes the period during which agreed updates
      will be provided, and the business has told the consumer of an update's availability and the
      consequences of not installing it, with adequate installation instructions, before relying on
      the consumer's own failure to update as a defence (DE: § 475b Abs. 3 Nr. 2, Abs. 5 BGB)
- [ ] No liability-exclusion or liability-limitation clause, and no auction liability limit, is
      relied on where the seller fraudulently concealed the defect or gave its own Garantie on the
      item's condition (DE: § 444, § 445 BGB)
- [ ] No consumer-sale clause reinstates the usage-value offset that § 475 Abs. 3 strips from
      § 439 Abs. 6, reintroduces §§ 442, 445 or 447 Abs. 2, or shifts the return-shipping cost onto
      the consumer on withdrawal or damages instead of full performance (DE: § 475 Abs. 3, 7 BGB)
- [ ] No clause shortens the Ware-with-digital-elements update period below what the consumer can
      expect, excuses the two update-reliance conditions above, or shortens the continuous-provision
      minimum liability period below two years from delivery, outside the same two-step
      specifically-flagged, expressly-and-separately-agreed route (DE: § 475b Abs. 4, 5, § 475c
      Abs. 2, § 476 Abs. 1 Satz 2 BGB)
- [ ] No clause agreed before notice of a defect worsens a consumer's position under §§ 433 to 435,
      437, 439 to 441 and 443 or the digital-products subtitle, outside the same two openings
      (objective-requirement or update-period departure; limitation period down to two years, one
      year for used goods); neither opening survives once notice of an actual defect has reached the
      business, and this carve-out does not reach a damages clause, which §§ 307 to 309 govern on
      their own terms (DE: § 476 Abs. 1, 2, 3, 4 BGB)
- [ ] A seller's recourse claim against its own Lieferant for Nacherfüllung expenses is tracked and
      time-barred under its own rule, not the buyer-facing limitation period: it covers only a defect
      already present when risk passed to the seller itself, or a breach of the Ware-with-digital-
      elements updating duty, and lapses two years from delivery but not before two months after the
      seller satisfied the buyer's own claims (DE: § 445a Abs. 1, § 445b Abs. 1, 2, § 478 Abs. 1 BGB)
- [ ] A Garantieerklärung given to a consumer is drafted simply and comprehensibly, states the
      consumer's statutory rights are free of charge and unaffected, names the Garantiegeber's
      address, the procedure to assert it, the covered Ware, and the Garantie's duration and
      territorial scope, reaching the consumer on a durable medium no later than delivery (DE:
      § 479 Abs. 1, 2 BGB)
- [ ] Where a manufacturer has given a consumer a Haltbarkeitsgarantie, the consumer gets, during the
      stated period, a minimum Nacherfüllung claim directly against the manufacturer, running
      alongside the claim against the seller (DE: § 479 Abs. 3 BGB)
- [ ] The manufacturer repair-obligation subtitle switches on only where all three conditions are met
      together: the goods belong to a product group listed in Anhang II of Directive (EU) 2024/1799,
      a consumer bought them, and the consumer no longer has the § 437 remedies for them; once it
      applies, the manufacturer also publishes, on a freely accessible website, indicative prices for
      typical repairs, and gives repair information free of charge in an easily accessible, clear and
      comprehensible way (DE: § 479a, § 479d Abs. 1, 2 BGB)
- [ ] Where repairability requirements apply, a spare-parts or repair-tools pricing policy does not
      discourage repair, no hardware or software measure hinders repair without an actual
      legitimate and objective justification, and no manufacturer refuses repair solely because an
      earlier repair was done by another business (DE: § 479b Abs. 2 Satz 3, § 479c, § 479e BGB)
- [ ] Refund, reduction and withdrawal mechanics for a consumer sale: cure given free of charge
      with the seller's free choice of method once both repair and replacement clear the
      disproportionate-cost weighing; price reduction processed as a declaration, calculated in
      the defect-free-to-actual-value ratio, not as a self-executing adjustment; withdrawal or
      damages instead of full performance allowed without a prior cure deadline where any one of
      the five statutory trigger conditions is met (DE: § 439 Abs. 1, 2, 4; § 441 Abs. 1, 3, 4;
      § 475d Abs. 1, 2 BGB)

## Dispute resolution notice (VSBG)

- [ ] A trader that maintains a website or uses standard terms gives the standing § 36 notice:
      whether and to what extent it is willing or obliged to take part in proceedings before a
      consumer arbitration body, easily accessible, clear and comprehensible, in both places where
      they apply (website; alongside the standard terms, not merely published on the same site)
      (DE: § 36 Abs. 1, 2 VSBG)
- [ ] Where the trader has committed, or is legally obliged, to take part before a named consumer
      arbitration body, that body's address and website are named in both triggered locations, even
      for a trader otherwise exempt from the general Nr. 1 statement (DE: § 36 Abs. 1 Nr. 2, Abs. 3
      VSBG)
- [ ] Once an actual consumer-contract dispute has failed to settle, a separate, case-specific
      notice in text form names the competent consumer arbitration body, its address and website,
      and states the trader's willingness or obligation to take part before it; the standing § 36
      notice is not relied on to cover this step, and the notice is not given orally (DE: § 37
      Abs. 1, 2 VSBG)
- [ ] No reference to the EU Online Dispute Resolution platform, or citation of Regulation (EU)
      No 524/2013 as a live channel, remains in any document issued or updated from 20 July 2025
      onward, since that Regulation is repealed and the platform itself stopped taking new
      complaints on 20 March 2025 (DE/EU: Art. 1, Art. 2(1), 2(2) Regulation (EU) 2024/3228)

## Impressum and commercial communications (DDG)

- [ ] An Impressum page lists every § 5 Abs. 1 item that actually applies to the provider's legal
      form, licensing status, registration and business (name and address; for a legal person, its
      legal form, representative, and, if capital figures are stated, the capital and unpaid
      contributions; fast electronic contact including an email address; the supervisory authority
      where an authorization is needed; the register and registration number where entered; for a
      regulated profession, the chamber, title and professional-conduct rules; a held VAT ID or
      Economic ID; a winding-up statement for an AG, KGaA or GmbH in liquidation; for an audiovisual
      media service, the seat state and supervisory authorities), kept permanently available, easily
      recognizable and directly accessible (DE: § 5 Abs. 1 Nr. 1 to 8 DDG)
- [ ] The Impressum is not treated as complete by including only some of the applicable items, and
      is not split across pages with no clear link between them (DE: § 5 Abs. 1 DDG)
- [ ] A terms document may link to a separate Impressum page instead of repeating every item, so
      long as that page independently meets the easily-recognizable, directly-accessible standard
      (DE: § 5 Abs. 1 DDG)
- [ ] Every commercial communication is labelled as commercial and names the person it is made for,
      on the communication itself, not through a disclosure buried in unrelated terms; a sales
      promotion or prize draw is marked as such with its conditions stated easily accessibly, clearly
      and unambiguously (DE: § 6 Abs. 1 Nr. 1 to 4 DDG)
- [ ] Commercial email keeps the header and subject line free of anything that disguises or conceals
      the sender or the commercial character, judged by whether the design leaves the recipient
      without that information, or with misleading information, before they view the content, not
      merely by literal accuracy (DE: § 6 Abs. 2 DDG)
- [ ] A video-sharing platform offers uploaders a function to declare whether a video contains
      audiovisual commercial communication, and marks it once the platform has learned of that
      content learned from any source, including one beyond the declaration (DE: § 6 Abs. 3, 4 DDG)
- [ ] A video-sharing platform's user terms secure an effective, affirmatively accepted ban on
      posting audiovisual commercial communication that advertises tobacco products (§ 20
      Tabakerzeugnisgesetz) or advertises medicinal products in violation of § 10
      Heilmittelwerbegesetz; a general content policy or community guideline is not relied on to
      satisfy this (DE: § 11 Abs. 1, 2 DDG)
- [ ] A document does not claim § 5 or § 6 DDG exhausts the provider's information or communication
      duties, since both sections expressly leave other regimes, including further statutory
      information duties, the UWG and the Preisangabenverordnung, in place alongside them (DE: § 5
      Abs. 2, § 6 Abs. 5 DDG)

## Records

- [ ] The business can show, for a given counterparty and contract, that the § 305 Abs. 2 inclusion
      conditions were actually met, beyond the bare fact that the terms existed on a website at the
      time (DE:
      § 305 Abs. 2 BGB)
- [ ] The system can show, after the fact, exactly when a given digital product or Ware was made
      available or accessible to a specific consumer, since the trader carries the burden of
      proving supply or delivery happened at all (DE: § 327b Abs. 6 BGB)
- [ ] Records show what information or notice a given counterparty received and when, for any term
      relying on an exception, a weighing test, or a reasonableness standard in this checklist,
      since several invalidity rules turn on what was actually disclosed to that counterparty (DE:
      § 312m Abs. 2 BGB)
