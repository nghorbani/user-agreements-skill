# Prescribed wording

Law as of: 2026-10-20

Quoted from the official texts. Use these words exactly; do not translate them.

## EU

For a member state other than Germany, use that state's national version of the model (its transposition prescribes the wording in its language). The Directive's English and German texts are the directive-level models.

### PW-EU-1 order button wording (Art. 8(2) Directive 2011/83/EU)
> order with obligation to pay

Where it goes: the button or similar function activating a distance order under an electronic contract that places the consumer under an obligation to pay, labelled in an easily legible manner with nothing else besides this phrase or an equivalent.
Alternatives: the Article allows "a corresponding unambiguous formulation indicating that placing the order entails an obligation to pay the trader".

### PW-EU-2 withdrawal function label (Art. 11a(1) Directive 2011/83/EU)
> withdraw from contract here

Where it goes: the withdrawal function on the online interface of a distance contract concluded by means of an online interface, labelled in an easily legible way, continuously available throughout the withdrawal period, prominently displayed and easily accessible.
Alternatives: the Article allows "an unambiguous corresponding formulation".

### PW-EU-3 confirmation function label (Art. 11a(3) Directive 2011/83/EU)
> confirm withdrawal

Where it goes: the confirmation function that lets the consumer submit the completed online withdrawal statement to the trader, labelled in an easily legible manner with only this phrase or an equivalent.
Alternatives: the Article allows "an unambiguous corresponding formulation".

### PW-EU-11 right-to-erasure heading (Art. 17 GDPR)
> Right to erasure ('right to be forgotten')

German version (Art. 17 DSGVO):
> Recht auf Löschung („Recht auf Vergessenwerden")

Where it goes: the heading of the erasure-rights section in a privacy notice's rights list; use this exact title rather than a paraphrase, in the language the notice is written in.

### PW-EU-12 affirmative-consent sentence pattern (Art. 4(11), 7(2) GDPR; EDPB Guidelines 05/2020)
> I, hereby, consent to the processing of my data

Where it goes: the consent-request text or checkbox label where a document needs a specific first-person affirmative-consent phrase and no other wording is fixed by the Regulation itself; the Guidelines give this as a sentence that meets the clear-affirmative-action standard. An acknowledgment-style phrase that only states awareness, such as "I know that...", does not meet it and must not be used in its place.

## Germany

### PW-DE-1 order-submission button wording (§ 312j Abs. 3 Satz 2 BGB)
> zahlungspflichtig bestellen

Where it goes: the order-submission button for any consumer e-commerce contract that obliges payment, legibly, with nothing else on the button besides this phrase or an equally unambiguous formulation.
Alternatives: the statute allows "einer entsprechenden eindeutigen Formulierung".

### PW-DE-2 cancellation button wording (§ 312k Abs. 2 Satz 2 BGB)
> Verträge hier kündigen

Where it goes: the cancellation button (Kündigungsschaltfläche) on any website letting a consumer conclude a continuing paid contract, legibly, with nothing else on the button besides this phrase or an equally unambiguous formulation; permanently available, immediately and easily accessible.
Alternatives: the statute allows "einer entsprechenden eindeutigen Formulierung".

### PW-DE-3 cancellation confirmation button wording (§ 312k Abs. 2 Satz 3 Nr. 2 BGB)
> jetzt kündigen

Where it goes: the confirmation button on the cancellation confirmation page the Kündigungsschaltfläche leads to, legibly, with nothing else on the button besides this phrase or an equally unambiguous formulation, submitting the cancellation declaration.
Alternatives: the statute allows "einer entsprechenden eindeutigen Formulierung".

### PW-DE-4 withdrawal function label (§ 356a Abs. 1 Satz 2 BGB)
> Vertrag widerrufen

Where it goes: the withdrawal function on the online interface of any distance contract, legibly labelled with this phrase or another equally unambiguous formulation; unlike PW-DE-1 to PW-DE-3, the statute does not add the "mit nichts anderem als" exclusivity of §§ 312j and 312k BGB to these words, only the label-or-equivalent standard.
Alternatives: the statute allows "einer anderen gleichbedeutenden eindeutigen Formulierung".

### PW-DE-5 confirmation function label (§ 356a Abs. 3 Satz 2 BGB)
> Widerruf bestätigen

Where it goes: the confirmation function that actually transmits the withdrawal declaration, legibly labelled with this phrase or another equally unambiguous formulation; same lighter standard as PW-DE-4, without the "mit nichts anderem als" exclusivity of §§ 312j and 312k BGB wording in the statute.
Alternatives: the statute allows "einer anderen gleichbedeutenden eindeutigen Formulierung".

## US federal

### PW-US-FED-1 implied-warranty state-variation statement (16 C.F.R. 701.3(a)(7))
> Some States do not allow limitations on how long an implied warranty lasts, so the above limitation may not apply to you.

Where it goes: accompanies any limitation on the duration of an implied warranty that is disclosed on the face of the warranty under 15 U.S.C. 2308.

### PW-US-FED-2 consequential-damages state-variation statement (16 C.F.R. 701.3(a)(8))
> Some States do not allow the exclusion or limitation of incidental or consequential damages, so the above limitation or exclusion may not apply to you.

Where it goes: accompanies any exclusion or limitation of incidental or consequential damages; may be combined with PW-US-FED-1.

### PW-US-FED-3 legal-rights statement (16 C.F.R. 701.3(a)(9))
> This warranty gives you specific legal rights, and you may also have other rights which vary from State to State.

Where it goes: every written warranty covered by part 701.

### PW-US-FED-4 full/limited designation wording (15 U.S.C. 2303(a)(1), (2))
> full (statement of duration) warranty

> limited warranty

Where it goes: the conspicuous caption or title designating a written warranty, clearly set apart from the warranty text; the first applies where the warranty meets the Federal minimum standards of 15 U.S.C. 2304, the second where it does not. ## US states

New York prescribes required content, not words (N.Y. Gen. Bus. Law § 527-a).

### PW-US-CA-1 phone cancellation trigger phrase (Cal. Bus. and Prof. Code § 17602(e)(1))
> cancel

Where it goes: the fixed trigger phrase a phone agent must disclose to the consumer, before presenting any retention offer or cancellation-effect information, as the way to complete cancellation at any time; once the consumer says it (or words to that effect), the agent must process the cancellation promptly.
Alternatives: the statute allows the consumer to state their intention to cancel "or words to that effect".

### PW-US-CA-2 online cancellation link or button label (Cal. Bus. and Prof. Code § 17602(e)(2))
> click to cancel

Where it goes: the label on the online cancellation link or button that must be shown prominently and continuously, in proximity to any retention offer or cancellation-effect information displayed during an online cancellation attempt; using it must promptly trigger cancellation.
Alternatives: the statute allows "words to that effect".

### PW-US-CA-3 opt-out link titles (Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.135(a)(1), (2))
> Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

> Limit the Use of My Sensitive Personal Information

Where it goes: two clear and conspicuous links on the business's internet homepages, each titled exactly as above, for a business that sells or shares personal information or uses sensitive personal information beyond the purposes the statute authorizes; each links to a page letting the consumer exercise that one right.

### PW-US-CA-4 alternative opt-out link title (7 CCR § 7015(b))
> Your Privacy Choices

or
> Your California Privacy Choices

Where it goes: the single, clearly labeled link a business may post instead of the two separate links at PW-US-CA-3, with the regulation's opt-out icon shown next to the title, approximately the same size as the business's other header or footer icons; the icon's colors may be adjusted only to keep it visible against the page background.

### PW-US-CA-5 deidentified patient information contract clauses (Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.148(c))
> (1) A statement that the deidentified information being sold or licensed includes deidentified patient information. (2) A statement that reidentification, and attempted reidentification, of the deidentified information by the purchaser or licensee of the information is prohibited pursuant to this section. (3) A requirement that, unless otherwise required by law, the purchaser or licensee of the deidentified information may not further disclose the deidentified information to any third party unless the third party is contractually bound by the same or stricter restrictions and conditions.

Where it goes: a contract for the sale or license of deidentified information that meets § 1798.146(a)(4), where a party resides or does business in California; the contract includes these three provisions or ones substantially similar to them.
