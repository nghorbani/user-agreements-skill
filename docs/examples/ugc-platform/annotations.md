## Part D: Gaps identified during the checklist run

- **VSBG § 36 standing dispute-resolution notice**: the German AGB checklist flags this as a standing duty for a trader maintaining a website or standard terms, independent of whether Shutterline has ever had an actual consumer dispute. This was missing from the first draft and has been added to both the Terms of Service (§14) and the Impressum, as a placeholder stating non-participation, since the content of that notice depends on a commercial decision (whether Lumenfolk in fact wants to participate in such proceedings) that the client has not stated. Confirm the actual position before publication; if Lumenfolk wants to participate, the placeholder is replaced with the named body's address and website, not merely a different sentence.
- **Designated DMCA agent and Impressum register/VAT details**: both are placeholders pending data the client has not yet supplied (see open point 10 and the Impressum document itself).
