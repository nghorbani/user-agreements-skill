This notice explains what personal data Shutterline collects, why, on what legal basis, who sees it, how long we keep it, and what rights you have over it. It covers users in the EU and the United States; where a right or a rule differs by where you live, that is stated in its own section below.

## 1. What we collect and why

| Category of data | Purpose | Legal basis (EU) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account data (email, birth date, display name, password) | Create and secure your account; confirm you meet our minimum age | Performance of our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) |
| Photos, comments and profile content you post | Operate the public-facing parts of Shutterline (feed, profile, search) | Performance of our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) |
| Direct messages | Deliver and store messages between you and another user | Performance of our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) |
| Activity data (what you view, like, and how long you spend on content) used to show you **plain contextual advertising** | Show contextual advertising next to the feed, unrelated to your personal profile | Our legitimate interest in funding a free service through advertising that does not use your personal profile (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) |
| Activity data (what you view, like and interact with) used to show you **advertising personalised to your activity** | Show you advertising selected based on your own behaviour on Shutterline | **Your consent** (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). See our [Cookie Notice] for how we ask for this. |
| Reports you submit, and reports submitted about you | Run our content-moderation process | Our legitimate interest in keeping Shutterline safe and lawful, and, where the report concerns a legal claim, our legal obligation to act on it (Art. 6(1)(c), (f) GDPR) |
| Technical and device data (IP address, browser type, device identifiers) | Operate, secure and troubleshoot Shutterline | Our legitimate interest in running a secure, working service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) |

A privacy notice for a free, advertising-funded service cannot rely on legitimate interest for personalised advertising built from your own behaviour and interests: you cannot reasonably expect us to process your personal data for personalised advertising without being asked first, so the basis for that specific purpose is your consent, not our legitimate interest. Plain contextual advertising that does not use a behavioural profile is a separate purpose, with a separate (legitimate-interest) basis, because it does not carry the same surprise or intrusion.

## 4. Your rights (EU users)

You have the right to:

- **access** the personal data we hold about you;
- ask us to **rectify** inaccurate data;
- ask us to **erase** your data, in the circumstances the law sets out;
- ask us to **restrict** our processing, in the circumstances the law sets out;
- **object** to processing based on our legitimate interest, on grounds relating to your particular situation; and
- **object to direct marketing**, including related profiling, at any time, with no justification needed: we always honour this;
- ask for your data in a **portable**, machine-readable format, where the processing rests on your consent or our contract with you and happens by automated means;
- **withdraw consent** at any time, as easily as you gave it, with no effect on the lawfulness of processing before you withdrew; and
- **complain** to a supervisory authority: in Germany, the data protection authority for North Rhine-Westphalia, or the authority in your own EU member state of residence.

To exercise a right, contact us at legal@lumenfolk.test. We respond within one month, extendable by two further months for a complex or numerous request, with a reason given to you within the first month. We do not charge for this unless your request is manifestly unfounded or excessive, and we carry the burden of showing that if we say so.

## 5. Consent, where consent is the basis

Where we ask for your consent (for example, for personalised advertising, or for a non-essential cookie), we ask separately for each purpose, in plain language, with a genuine option to decline that is as easy to use as the option to agree. You can withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it, through your [Privacy settings]; withdrawing does not affect anything we did before you withdrew, and does not cost you anything or reduce the rest of Shutterline beyond what that one withdrawn purpose enabled.
