# Scout Cloud -- Subscription Terms

Draft for counsel review. Not legal advice. Law as of 20 October 2026.

Scout Cloud is an optional add-on plan for cloud photo storage, EUR 4.99 or USD 4.99 per month, billed monthly and renewing automatically until cancelled. No free trial. These terms apply only to the Scout Cloud subscription; the camera, the desktop App and the Firmware are covered by the separate end user licence agreement.

**Keep only the module for each subscriber's actual status; do not blend modules into one document served to everyone.**

---

By [clicking the button labeled "Subscribe" / continuing to complete your Scout Cloud sign-up], you agree to these Scout Cloud terms and to our privacy notice.

[Link to the full terms, placed immediately next to this sentence and the control it names, in a colour and weight that contrasts with the surrounding text.]

Before you complete your sign-up, you can review and change your plan and your payment method. Once you submit, we will send you an electronic confirmation to the email address on your account.

**EU consumers.** Immediately before the order button, and with nothing else between the two, add the four-point disclosure: the technical steps to conclude the contract, whether we file the concluded contract and whether you can access it, the technical means to spot and correct an input error before you submit, and the languages the contract can be concluded in (Art. 10(1)(a) to (d) Directive 2000/31/EC).

**Germany.** The order button carries only PW-DE-1 ("zahlungspflichtig bestellen") or an equally unambiguous equivalent, with nothing else on it; the price (EUR 4.99 per month), the plan's main characteristics, that it renews monthly, and the rest of the EGBGB Art. 246a list sit directly before that button, highlighted, not behind a link (§ 312j Abs. 2, 3 BGB).

**US.** Place the assent sentence on the same screen as the control it names, in a size and colour comparably prominent to the surrounding text, directly next to the control, visible without scrolling. Do not use a pre-checked assent box.

## Module: term, renewal and cancellation

This subscription starts on the date we confirm your sign-up and continues for one month. Unless you cancel before the end of that month, it then renews automatically for successive one-month periods, at the price and on the terms in effect at the time of each renewal, until you cancel or we end it as this clause allows.

Before you are charged for the first time, and before each renewal, we will show you, or send you, the price you will be charged, how often you will be charged, and how to cancel.

You can cancel at any time. Cancelling stops the next renewal; it does not refund a period that has already started. There is no free trial on Scout Cloud, so this clause does not need a trial-conversion notice.

**EU consumers.** `references/eu/uctd.md` sets the EU-wide floor: an automatic renewal needs a reasonable, not unreasonably early, objection deadline, and cancellation is at least as easy as sign-up (UCTD Annex point (h)). **EU business customers.** The references do not set a business-specific renewal or cancellation position; treat a business-facing (reserve/farm) renewal and cancellation clause as an open point for counsel. **Germany.** Because Scout Cloud is a monthly continuing obligation on preformulated terms, state the renewal mechanism so it extends the contract onto an indefinite term, not a further fixed term, and gives you a contractual right to end the renewed contract at any time on no more than one month's notice (§ 309 Nr. 9 Buchst. b BGB).

Provide a cancellation button, permanently available, immediately and easily accessible, labelled PW-DE-2 ("Verträge hier kündigen"), routing directly to a confirmation page labelled with the PW-DE-3 ("jetzt kündigen") confirmation button (§ 312k Abs. 1, 2 BGB). The confirmation page asks only for: cancellation type; data identifying you; data identifying the contract; an optional end date you are never required to fill in; and an electronic channel for the confirmation (§ 312k Abs. 2 Satz 3 Nr. 1 Buchst. a to e BGB). No login step sits between the button and the confirmation page [pending the BGH's 5 November 2026 ruling -- see `open-points.md`, OP-19]. You can store your own cancellation declaration on a durable medium, time-stamped. We confirm receipt immediately, electronically, in text form, stating the content, the time stamp, and the resulting end date. A missing or non-compliant button or confirmation page lets you cancel at any time without any notice period (§ 312k Abs. 6 BGB). The confirmation page carries no retention offer, discount pitch, or pause option (§ 312k Abs. 2 Satz 3 BGB; BGH, judgment of 16 July 2026, I ZR 200/25).

**US.** Before taking your billing information, we disclose, in visual proximity to the control that records your consent: that the plan continues until cancelled, the cancellation mechanism, the charge (USD 4.99 per month) and whether and how it may change, that the renewal term is monthly, and that there is no minimum-purchase obligation (Cal. Bus. and Prof. Code § 17601(a)(2)(A) to (E); N.Y. Gen. Bus. Law § 527-a(1)(a)). We obtain your affirmative consent to the offer terms before the first charge and keep a verifiable record of it for at least three years, or one year after the contract ends, whichever is longer (Cal. Bus. and Prof. Code § 17602(a)(2), (a)(4), (a)(6)). After consent, we send a retainable acknowledgment covering the offer terms, the cancellation policy, and how to cancel (Cal. Bus. and Prof. Code § 17602(a)(3); N.Y. Gen. Bus. Law § 527-a(1)(c)). We describe the cancellation mechanism as [an in-app cancellation control / an account-settings page], reachable through at least the same medium you used to consent (Cal. Bus. and Prof. Code § 17602(f); N.Y. Gen. Bus. Law § 527-a(1)(d), (d-1)). If online acceptance is offered, we add no step beyond permitted account authentication that delays online cancellation (Cal. Bus. and Prof. Code § 17602(d)(1)). Where a material change, including a fee change, is made, we give clear, conspicuous, retainable notice with cancellation instructions 7 to 30 days before it takes effect in California (Cal. Bus. and Prof. Code § 17602(g)(2)) and 5 business days to 30 days before it takes effect in New York (N.Y. Gen. Bus. Law § 527-a(1)(g)); in New York, on a price increase, we either capture your affirmative consent to the new price before charging it, or let you cancel within at least fourteen days after the charge with a pro rata refund (N.Y. Gen. Bus. Law § 527-a(1)(b-1)). Nothing here interferes with, detracts from, or contradicts your ability to give affirmative consent to renewal, and we do not misrepresent whether the plan renews automatically (Cal. Bus. and Prof. Code § 17602(a)(5), (a)(7)). A discount, retention benefit, or cancellation-effect information is shown only together with the required cancellation trigger, never instead of it or ahead of it (Cal. Bus. and Prof. Code § 17602(e)(1), (e)(2); N.Y. Gen. Bus. Law § 527-a(1)(e)(i), (e)(ii)).

Where a change is more than a minor clarification, correction of an error, or adjustment required to keep Scout Cloud lawful, safe or secure, we will tell you about it in advance, state the reason for the change and when it takes effect, and give you a reasonable period before the change applies. Where the change is more than a minor negative effect on your access to or use of Scout Cloud, you may end the contract free of charge within the notice period described above. A change does not apply to a dispute that already exists between you and us before the change takes effect.

**EU consumers.** A change is permitted only where this contract itself allows it and states a valid reason, the change carries no extra cost for you, you are told about it clearly, and, where it does more than minimally and negatively affect your access or use, you are told reasonably in advance, on a durable medium, with at least 30 days to terminate free of charge or keep the unmodified version instead (Art. 19 Directive (EU) 2019/770). **Germany.** A clause letting us change these terms, including price, through your silence or continued use, with no substantive limit on what can be changed, is invalid, even where a matching special termination right is also offered (BGH, judgment of 27 April 2021, XI ZR 26/20). **US.** No change applies to a dispute that has already accrued before the change takes effect, and no change binds a person who has only browsed Scout Cloud without subscribing (Heckman v. Live Nation Entertainment, Inc., 120 F.4th 670).

Using this function needs you to give or confirm your name, the details identifying this subscription, and a way of reaching you to confirm the withdrawal. ## Module: what Scout Cloud delivers (warranty and conformity)

Fernlook supplies Scout Cloud so that it matches what this contract describes and promises, and, on top of that, has the quantity, quality, and performance a consumer may reasonably expect for a cloud storage service of this kind, including its usual functionality, availability and security.

If Scout Cloud does not match what this clause promises, you can ask Fernlook to fix it; Fernlook has a free choice of how to bring it into conformity. The fix is free of charge, happens within a reasonable time, and causes you no significant inconvenience. If the fix does not happen in time, fails, or Fernlook refuses or will clearly not do it, you can ask instead for a price reduction, or can end the contract unless the problem is only minor.

**EU consumers.** State the liability period as at least two years from supply, extended to the whole period Scout Cloud continues to be supplied, since this is continuous supply (Art. 11(2), (3) Directive (EU) 2019/770). An interruption of more than a negligible time, or one that recurs, already counts as a lack of conformity; this clause does not define conformity so that only a total outage counts (recital 51). Termination is unavailable for a merely minor lack of conformity, and the burden of proving the shortfall is minor sits with Fernlook (Art. 14(6)). **Germany.** Use the German terms for the two conformity layers (§ 327e Abs. 2 BGB). State the limitation start as the end of the supply period for continuous supply (§ 327j Abs. 2 BGB). **US.** Fernlook does not issue a separate written warranty for Scout Cloud; this conformity statement is not itself a Magnuson-Moss written warranty, since it carries no tied performance level over a stated time beyond the service description itself.

## Module: liability and disclaimers

Nothing in these terms limits or excludes a liability that the law does not let Fernlook limit or exclude. In particular, nothing here limits Fernlook's liability for death or personal injury, for its own fraud or intentional wrongdoing, for a grossly negligent breach of duty, for a negligent breach of a duty essential to this contract, or for liability a specific statute states cannot be contracted away.

Outside what the paragraph above protects, Fernlook's liability for a negligent breach of a duty that is not essential to this contract is limited to the loss that was foreseeable and ordinary for a subscription of this kind. Fernlook is not liable for indirect or consequential loss, including lost profit or lost photos, except where the paragraph above says this clause cannot limit that liability.

**EU consumers.** State the floor first: nothing reaches liability for death or personal injury caused by Fernlook's own act or omission, or unduly excludes your legal rights for non-performance (Annex points (a), (b) UCTD). **Germany.** No clause excludes or limits liability for death, personal injury, or harm to health arising from a negligent breach by Fernlook, or an intentional or negligent breach by its staff or agents, with no carve-out for any contract type (§ 309 Nr. 7 Buchst. a BGB). **US.** Draft a conspicuous as-is/as-available disclaimer and a liability cap (a stated dollar figure, or the subscription fees paid in a defined period, whichever is greater), paired with a disclaimer of incidental and consequential damages. For a California subscriber, no release, disclaimer or limitation clause may exempt Fernlook from its own fraud, from willful injury, or from a violation of law (Cal. Civ. Code § 1668).

Fernlook may transfer this agreement to another entity, for example as part of a merger, acquisition, reorganisation or sale of some or all of its assets. If a court or authority finds that a part of these terms is not valid or not enforceable, the rest of these terms stays in effect. These terms are the entire agreement between you and Fernlook about Scout Cloud, and replace any earlier agreement or statement about that subject matter.

**EU consumers.** State that a notice or confirmation required by this agreement is given on a durable medium; a bare link to a webpage is never a durable medium on its own (Art. 2(10) Directive 2011/83/EU; Case C-49/11, Content Services). Where a standard term is invalid, the rest of the contract stays valid and the gap is filled by statutory law. **US.** A contract, signature, notice or other record is not denied legal effect only because it is in electronic form.
