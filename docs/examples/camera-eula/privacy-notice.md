## What we collect, why, and for how long -- short version

Fernlook Scout is a Wi-Fi connected camera. Whenever your camera is online, it sends us diagnostic and usage data about the camera itself: device temperature, battery health, Wi-Fi signal quality, firmware version, and crash logs. This is separate from your wildlife photos, which stay on your own storage or in Scout Cloud if you subscribe. We do not sell this diagnostic data. The full detail, including every legal basis, every recipient, and how long each category is kept, is below, together with how to exercise your rights.

## Your rights (EU and Germany)

You can ask us to:

- confirm whether we process your data and give you access to it (Art. 15 GDPR);
- correct inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR);
- **Right to erasure ('right to be forgotten')** -- ask us to erase your data in the situations the law sets out (Art. 17 GDPR);
- restrict processing in specific situations (Art. 18 GDPR);
- receive the data you gave us in a portable format, where the processing rests on your consent or on a contract and happens by automated means (Art. 20 GDPR);
- object to processing that rests on our legitimate interest, including the diagnostic-data processing described above (Art. 21(1) GDPR).

**Direct marketing:** you can object to marketing at any time, free of charge, with no need to justify the objection, and we stop using your data for that purpose once you do (Art. 21(2), (3) GDPR). [We do not currently send marketing communications based on the facts given; if Fernlook plans to, this section needs its own consent/opt-out mechanics -- see `references/clauses/marketing-communications.md`, not drafted here as out of scope of the documents requested.]

**Complaints:** our lead supervisory authority is the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens). You can lodge a complaint with it, bring a judicial remedy against it, or bring a separate judicial remedy directly against us; these are three separate routes, and you do not need to use one before another (Art. 77, 78, 79 GDPR).

**How to reach us:** legal@fernlook-optics.test. We answer within one month, extendable by two further months for complex or numerous requests, and we will tell you within the first month if we need the extension and why (Art. 12(3) GDPR). If we will not act on your request, we tell you within one month, with reasons, and tell you about your right to complain to the supervisory authority and to seek a judicial remedy (Art. 12(4) GDPR). This channel is free; we only charge a fee or refuse where your request is manifestly unfounded or excessive, and the burden of showing that is on us (Art. 12(5) GDPR). We only ask you to verify your identity where we have reasonable doubt about it (Art. 12(6) GDPR).
