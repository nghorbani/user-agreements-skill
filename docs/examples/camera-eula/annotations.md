## Not drafted / out of scope

- A data processing agreement was not drafted; see `reply.md` and open point OP-2 for why, and the condition under which one would be added.
- A cookie notice for Fernlook's website was not requested and is not drafted; the ePrivacy/TDDDG rows above are flagged, not resolved.
- An acceptable use policy was not requested (the product has no user-generated content or platform feature) and is not drafted.
- Terms of service for the web store checkout generally (as distinct from the EULA and Scout Cloud terms) were not separately requested; the EULA's acceptance clause covers the camera purchase flow, but a standalone "terms of service" document for the web store itself (returns policy, shipping terms, etc., beyond the conformity and withdrawal rules already stated) was not requested and is not drafted.
