Draft for counsel review. Not legal advice. Law as of 20 October 2026.

Covers: the Fernlook Scout desktop companion app (Windows and macOS) and the camera firmware. This is a one-off hardware purchase (the camera) with a perpetual software licence included in the purchase price, plus firmware updates. It does not cover Scout Cloud, which has its own terms (`scout-cloud-terms.md`).

**This document uses bracketed modules for EU consumers, EU business customers, Germany, and US consumers/businesses. Publish the module that matches each buyer's actual status; do not blend modules in one document served to everyone.**

**EU consumers:** state both conformity layers together: the contract's own promise (description, quantity, quality, stated purpose, agreed accessories, instructions and updates) and the objective floor that applies regardless of the contract's wording (normal use, usual durability, the qualities a consumer may reasonably expect) (Art. 5 to 9 Directive (EU) 2019/771 for the camera as goods with digital elements; Art. 6 to 9 Directive (EU) 2019/770 for the App as digital content). The camera's firmware is a digital element of goods within the sales contract, not a separate digital-content contract, so the seller's duty to supply conformity and security updates sits inside this one conformity clause (SGD Art. 2(5)(b), 3(3), 7(3)). State the liability period as at least two years from delivery (Art. 10(1) Directive (EU) 2019/771). Because this contract is concluded for the March 2027 launch, which is after 31 July 2026, add that choosing repair as the remedy extends the liability period once by 12 months, and disclose that possible extension, together with your choice between repair and replacement, before the remedy is carried out (Art. 10(2a), 13(2a), as amended by Directive (EU) 2024/1799). Give the consumer the choice between repair and replacement for the camera as goods; decline the choice only where it is impossible or disproportionate (Art. 13(2), (3)).

**Germany:** use the German terms for the two conformity layers (vertraglich vereinbarte Beschaffenheit and the objective requirements) (§ 434 Abs. 1 to 3 BGB for the camera; § 327e Abs. 2 BGB for the App). Before Nacherfüllung, tell the consumer of the choice between repair and replacement and that choosing repair extends the limitation period once by 12 months (§ 475 Abs. 4 BGB). State the limitation period as two years from delivery for the general case (§ 438 Abs. 1 Nr. 3 BGB), with the five further consumer-sale floors in § 475e BGB layered on top where they apply (twelve months after continuous digital-element provision ends; twelve months after an update-duty period ends; four months after a defect first shows up within the general period; two months after a cured item is handed back; a one-time twelve-month extension once Nacherfüllung is rendered by repair).

**EU consumers and EU business customers:** give the Art. 3(2) disclosures (type, format and estimated volume of data the camera can generate; that it generates continuously and in real time while online; whether and how long it is stored on-device or remotely; how you can access, retrieve or erase it) before the contract for the camera is concluded, not folded into this agreement after the fact (Regulation (EU) 2023/2854, Art. 3(2)(a) to (d)).
